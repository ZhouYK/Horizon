It shows that local multiplayer between two arbitrary phones can work without Wi-Fi, Bluetooth pairing, servers or installs — only a browser, a speaker and a microphone — which is a notable trick for offline demos, classrooms and event games. The design depends on the two players holding their phones near each other, granting microphone permission, and having working speakers, so loud ambient noise, room echo, audio latency and differing hardware quality are real constraints; the author notes the audio approach simply &quot;seems to work&quot; rather than being measured for reliability or throughput.

Claude Code is Anthropic&\#x27;s agentic coding tool that lives in the terminal and can read a codebase, write files and run commands from natural-language instructions, which is how this game was built.
