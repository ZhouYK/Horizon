## [New Design Creativity Bench finds AI far behind humans in UI creativity](https://kombai.com/benchmark/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A new benchmark called Design Creativity Bench was released to measure creativity in LLM-generated UI designs. In its evaluation, GPT-6 Astra led all AI models with a score of 65.5, while human designers scored 92.3, although the best AI models slightly beat humans on requirements coverage, hitting 99.2% versus 98.0% for humans. The result shows that even as LLMs saturate traditional capability evaluations, they still lag badly on the creative aspects of design work, which matters for anyone building or using AI design tools. It suggests current models are better at executing a specified brief than at generating original visual direction, pointing to a human-in-the-loop role for designers rather than outright replacement. The benchmark separates three signals: cross-model originality \(distinctiveness among models given the same prompt\), creative range \(how much a model&\#x27;s designs change between two related briefs\), and acceptance-criteria coverage. It combines rendered UI segments with a separate colour score to quantify how strongly models repeat the same design decisions across tasks.

**Background**: Design Creativity Bench is a benchmark introduced by Kombai \(with an accompanying arXiv paper\) that evaluates diversity and appropriateness in UI designs generated by large language models. GPT-6 Astra is OpenAI&\#x27;s reasoning model released in September 2026, billed as its most capable model for coding, computer use and agentic workflows, and it is the top-scoring AI system in this test. Measuring machine creativity is inherently difficult because originality is subjective, so benchmarks like this rely on proxy signals such as output distinctiveness and requirement coverage rather than a single human judgement.

## [Cook: Apple Intelligence China Beta Lands Late October, Without Siri AI](https://jiemian.com/article/15194723.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

On October 15, Apple executive chairman Tim Cook said in an interview in Shanghai that Apple Intelligence and Alibaba&\#x27;s Qwen large model have received approval, and a beta version will be released in China later in October. The first batch of features includes live translation, writing tools and a photo clean-up tool, but Siri AI is not part of this release. This marks Apple&\#x27;s first real entry into the Chinese generative AI market after a long regulatory and partnership standoff, and it makes Alibaba&\#x27;s Qwen the model layer behind Apple Intelligence on the mainland. It matters to hundreds of millions of iPhone users in China, to Alibaba&\#x27;s positioning against domestic rivals such as DeepSeek and ByteDance, and to Apple&\#x27;s efforts to defend its premium smartphone share in a market where on-device AI is becoming a buying criterion. The launch is a beta, not a full general release, and Siri AI — the assistant overhaul Apple has been promoting globally — is explicitly excluded from the first Chinese version. Apple Intelligence combines on-device processing with server-side models, so the China build requires a locally approved model, which is where Qwen comes in; no pricing, device eligibility list or exact date was disclosed.

## [Google rolls out new AI-powered anti-scam features on Android](https://blog.google/security/new-android-scam-protections/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Google announced a set of AI-driven anti-scam protections for Android, led by an Enhanced Protection Mode in Google Messages that uses privacy-preserving cloud computation to call the Gemini model for analyzing the scam intent of conversations with unknown senders, paired with on-device Gemini Nano to check unknown links in real time. The update also extends automatic password and payment-page masking during screen sharing to Chrome and WebView web components, and adds background blocking of call forwarding plus warnings for manual forwarding setup to stop one-time passcodes from being stolen. Scam calls, texts and screen-sharing fraud are among the fastest-growing mobile threats, so baking layered AI defenses directly into the Android platform and its default messaging app reaches hundreds of millions of users without requiring them to install anything. It also shows Google&\#x27;s dual-track AI strategy in practice — cloud Gemini for deep reasoning and on-device Gemini Nano for privacy-sensitive, low-latency tasks — which could set expectations for how other platform vendors handle consumer security. The message analysis is described as running through privacy-preserving computation that sends data to the cloud Gemini model rather than processing everything locally, while link checking stays on-device with Gemini Nano, so device support and hardware capability will determine which protections users actually get. Call-forwarding protection is reported to arrive starting in early December 2026 on Pixel and other select devices, meaning availability is staged and not universal at launch.

**Background**: Gemini Nano is Google&\#x27;s smallest foundation model, designed to run natively on Android devices through the AICore system service so that tasks like text summarization or link checking work without a network connection and without sending user data off the device. Cloud-based Gemini, by contrast, runs in Google&\#x27;s data centers and can handle more complex reasoning, but requires connectivity and careful privacy handling. Screen-sharing masking builds on Android&\#x27;s existing tools such as the FLAG\_SECURE window flag, which prevents sensitive windows from appearing in screenshots or casts; extending this to Chrome and WebView means masking now covers content rendered inside web pages, not just native app UI. Call forwarding is a common scam vector because attackers trick victims into forwarding calls so that one-time passcodes for banking or account logins are delivered to the fraudster&\#x27;s phone.

## [China Accelerates Removal of Western Wind Power Equipment for Energy Security](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-15/china-removes-western-wind-power-equipment-to-boost-security?srnd=phx-technology) ⭐️ 6.0/10

China is accelerating the replacement of Western-made wind power equipment in its energy infrastructure, focusing on turbine control systems, industrial software and related components. Poland&\#x27;s Centre for Eastern Studies, reviewing more than 1,600 public procurement documents, found nearly 300 cases involving the removal or substitution of foreign equipment. The shift elevates energy security above cost and efficiency in procurement decisions, potentially opening new market space for domestic Chinese wind equipment makers while squeezing Western suppliers such as European control-system and software vendors out of a shrinking share of the world&\#x27;s largest wind market. It also marks another front in the broader technology decoupling between China and the West, extending beyond semiconductors into industrial automation and clean-energy infrastructure. Chinese energy companies justify the swap by citing high maintenance costs for imported equipment, long spare-parts lead times and perceived security risks from foreign technology. The finding is drawn from an analysis of public procurement records rather than a single official policy announcement, so it reflects a documented trend across many tenders rather than one coordinated mandate.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 15, 14:55

**Background**: Wind turbine control systems are the hardware and software that govern a turbine&\#x27;s pitch angle, yaw direction and power output, and they are typically bundled with industrial software such as SCADA platforms that monitor and report on whole wind farms. Historically, European and other Western vendors supplied much of this control and monitoring technology to Chinese wind projects, making the control layer a sensitive point if suppliers were ever cut off. Replacing that layer with domestic alternatives is therefore framed as a reliability and national-security measure as much as a commercial one.
