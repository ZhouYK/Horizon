The feature debuts on September 3 at 9:00 p.m. PT in NBA 2K27, available on GeForce RTX 50-series PCs and laptops and through GeForce NOW Ultimate. telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 2, 03:00

**Background**: DLSS \(Deep Learning Super Sampling\) has traditionally used dedicated Tensor cores to upscale lower-resolution images, generate additional frames, or clean up ray-traced lighting. In DLSS 5, a generative AI model works as a final stage after the game engine draws a frame, essentially repainting how that frame responds to light and restoring detail that real-time performance limits previously forced developers to remove.

High school students will only be allowed to use these tools under limited conditions. As the largest U.S. school district, New York City&\#x27;s policy could influence other districts wrestling with AI in education. Teachers may still use approved AI for lesson planning, translation, and writing notices, but are prohibited from using it for grading, behavior monitoring, crisis counseling, or developing special education plans.

## [Mac App Store Apps Can Drop Intel Support; Tahoe Is Last Intel macOS](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/01/mac-app-store-intel-mac-support/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Apple has notified developers that Mac App Store universal apps requiring macOS 13 or later may now drop Intel Mac support. After doing so, Intel Macs will no longer receive app updates, though users can continue using the last compatible version. This policy change simplifies Universal App development and reduces download and storage size, and is a clear signal that Apple is ending Intel Mac support in its App Store ecosystem. It also lets developers fully target Apple Silicon as macOS Tahoe becomes the final release that supports Intel Macs. The change applies only to universal apps with a deployment target of macOS 13 or higher; older apps and those targeting earlier OS versions are unaffected. Dropping Intel support optimizes app download size and on-device footprint.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 2, 03:30

**Background**: A Mac universal app contains binaries for both Intel and Apple Silicon, allowing it to run natively on either architecture. Apple began transitioning Macs from Intel to its own Apple Silicon chips in 2020, and developers have increasingly shipped universal apps during the transition. According to this report, macOS Tahoe is the last macOS version that supports Intel Macs, so Apple is now aligning App Store policy with that hardware roadmap.

## [China&\#x27;s Regulator Clears 5.61 Million Problematic AI Items in Cleanup Push](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-09/02/c_1790099041364574.htm) ⭐️ 6.0/10

China&\#x27;s Cyberspace Administration \(CAC\) announced that the second phase of its &\#x27;Qinglang: Rectifying AI Application Chaos&\#x27; campaign removed more than 5.61 million pieces of problematic AI-related information. Major platforms — including Douyin, Kuaishou, Weibo, Tencent, Baidu, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu, Douban and Taobao — issued 46 governance notices and deployed upgraded multimodal content-detection tools. The action signals tightening regulatory oversight of AI applications at an industrial scale in China, directly affecting platform content moderation and AI service providers. It reinforces that Chinese tech companies must build compliance mechanisms — such as synthetic-content labeling and app-store vetting — into their AI products. As part of the second phase, generative AI platforms such as Doubao, Yuanbao, Qianwen and Wenxin Yiyan must strictly audit source corpora, restrict disallowed outputs, and implement mandatory AI-generated content labeling. App stores operated by Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO and vivo tightened developer admission rules and performed random re-inspections of listed apps to block non-compliant software.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 2, 05:24

**Background**: The campaign falls under China&\#x27;s &\#x27;Qinglang&\#x27; \(Clear and Bright\) series of internet content governance actions. The first phase targeted typical violations in AI application services, while this second phase focuses on cleaning up AI-related information chaos; the CAC also runs a dedicated portal to receive public reports. Chinese regulators have introduced mandatory standards requiring AI-generated synthetic content to carry explicit labels and implicit metadata identifiers, which platforms began enforcing on September 1, 2025. Multimodal recognition models, which combine inputs such as images, audio and text, are a key technology platforms are using to detect harmful content.

## [China&\#x27;s New National Standard Requires Clear Human-Agent Option in AI Customer Service](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Agt4qI5tgQA4kCT1DJX6fg) ⭐️ 6.0/10

China&\#x27;s first national standard focused on human-AI customer service collaboration, GB/T 47746—2026, took effect on September 1. It requires platforms to provide an obvious transfer-to-human-agent option and holds enterprises responsible for the content of AI customer service replies. The rule directly targets the common complaint that users get trapped in AI customer-service loops and cannot reach a human agent. It gives Chinese regulators and mediators a formal reference point, pressuring platforms to balance cost-saving AI automation with consumer-rights protection. The standard is a recommendatory national standard \(GB/T\), not mandatory, but it can be cited in supervision and dispute resolution. Context cited in the report: AI customer service can cost as little as 99 yuan a month, while a single human agent in a first-tier city may cost 80,000–120,000 yuan a year.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 2, 11:17

**Background**: Businesses increasingly deploy AI customer-service systems for e-commerce, telecom and after-sales support because they are cheap and easy to scale. A common consumer complaint is that the menu path to a human agent is hidden or excessively long. To address this, national standard GB/T 47746—2026, published under China&\#x27;s recommendatory GB/T system, sets collaborative requirements for human and intelligent customer service and provides a common reference for regulators and mediators.
