## [Moody&\#x27;s: Developed-Market Government Bond Yields Surge, a Structural Shift Is Underway](https://international.caixin.com/2026-09-16/102485230.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

**Background**: The 10-year Treasury yield is the interest rate the US government pays to borrow for a decade, and it anchors pricing for virtually all other assets. ## [Pinglu Canal opens: 134-km waterway links southwest China&\#x27;s rivers to Beibu Gulf](https://photos.caixin.com/2026-09-16/102485302.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

On September 16, 2026, the 134.2-kilometre Pinglu Canal officially opened to navigation, running from the mouth of the Pingtang River at Hengzhou in Nanning, through Luwu town in Lingshan county, and down the Qin River into the Beibu Gulf. It is the first canal built since the founding of the People&\#x27;s Republic of China that directly connects an inland river system to the sea. The canal gives Guangxi and the wider southwest an outlet to the sea that bypasses the long detour east to Guangzhou and the Pearl River Delta, potentially reshaping regional logistics routes, port competition among Beibu Gulf and Pearl River Delta ports, and the siting of downstream industries. Because Beijing has elevated the project to a national flagship infrastructure programme, its completion is also a signal about the direction of China&\#x27;s inland-waterway and western-development investment. The canal takes its water from the Xijin hydropower reservoir, which sits at the Pingtang River mouth and whose dam blocks the Yu River at Hengzhou. Xijin, begun in October 1958 and generating its first unit in June 1964, was Guangxi&\#x27;s first self-designed and self-built medium hydropower station, then China&\#x27;s largest riverbed-type station with 234.4 MW of installed capacity, and it carries a lock able to pass 2×1,000-tonne barge fleets with a maximum annual throughput of about 6 million tonnes. The canal was fully filled with water in early June 2026 and entered commissioning tests before the September opening, and Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang attended the navigation ceremony.
