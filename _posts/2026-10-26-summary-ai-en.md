## [Microsoft Offers Voluntary Buyouts to 60,000 Employees Amid Surging AI Spending](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMixgFBVV95cUxObWNkNFVaSVBnVERoMW1waUE2d1kwblIxeGhWOVNfcmdhcDY4TTA0SlFlZWdMbVFXYUxHVzVHUy1iMk9laTFienNSem9zS2lsXzhMZ2N1TDg2N2U3V1Y4d1dwMkJtY3A5WVVyTVZMdzlDc2xjbkttQkwxZEg5N2UzZGtqZnRjbklNaVU5TF81RTZ2akFqMkVvMTNOWkhXS1hMNUUxdjIza0h0NjQ4YV9aTG1YZDRBaUZUMTFTR1lKQnBGV3cySnfSAcYBQVVfeXFMTm1jZDRVWklQZ1REaDFtcGlBNndZMG5SMXhoVjlTX3JnYXA2OE0wNEpRZWVnTG1RV2FMR1c1R1MtYjJPZWkxYnpzUnpvc0tpbF84TGdjdUw4NjdlN1dWOHdXcDJCbWNwOVlVck1WTHc5Q3NsY25LbUJMMWRIOTdlM2RramZ0Y25JTWlVOUxfNUU2dmpBajJFbzEzTlpIV0tYTDVFMXYyM2tIdDY0OGFfWkxtWGQ0QWlGVDExU0dZSkJwRld3Mkp3?oc=5) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Microsoft has offered voluntary buyout packages to roughly 60,000 employees — a large slice of its global workforce — as the company ramps up capital spending on artificial intelligence infrastructure. The move was reported by multiple outlets including The New York Times and CBS News, framing it as a cost-discipline step taken alongside, not instead of, aggressive AI investment. The buyouts signal that even the most profitable hyperscalers are reallocating headcount budget toward AI compute and datacenters, a pattern likely to pressure traditional software and operations roles across the tech industry. Because Microsoft is a bellwether employer, its restructuring is widely read as a template other large technology firms may follow. The offer is voluntary, meaning the 60,000 figure reflects eligible employees rather than confirmed departures, and Microsoft has not disclosed how many acceptances it expects or the cost of the packages. The company has not framed the program as layoffs, and details such as eligibility criteria, deadlines and payout formulas have not been fully public in the reporting.

**Background**: Microsoft&\#x27;s fiscal year begins in July, and the company has been signaling for several quarters that AI infrastructure — GPUs, datacenters and power — would absorb a growing share of its capital expenditure, which has climbed into the tens of billions of dollars annually. At the same time, its headcount has continued to grow modestly, creating tension between spending on people and spending on compute. A voluntary buyout is a common corporate tool for reducing headcount without forced layoffs: employees who meet criteria such as age plus years of service are offered a payout to leave. Microsoft previously conducted large layoffs in 2023, and the industry-wide pattern since then has been to trim mature product and support teams while hiring for AI roles.
