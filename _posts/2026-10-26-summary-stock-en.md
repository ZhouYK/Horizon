## [PBOC Launches BNSF Liquidity Backstop for Non-Bank Bond Market](https://finance.caixin.com/2026-10-26/102497522.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On October 26, 2026, the People&\#x27;s Bank of China formally established the Bond Market NBFI Swap Facility \(BNSF\), allowing systemically important bank wealth management subsidiaries and public fund managers to borrow central bank bills for repo financing and use the proceeds to buy eligible bonds or shares of their own redeemed public products, which are then pledged to the PBOC. This is the PBOC&\#x27;s first dedicated liquidity backstop for non-bank asset managers in the bond market, designed to break the redemption-fire-sale negative loop that can amplify systemic stress. It extends China&\#x27;s macroprudential framework beyond banks and could materially change bond-market liquidity conditions and how wealth management and fund products are risk-priced. The facility is meant to be activated only in specific scenarios of herd liquidity risk among asset managers and systemic stress in the bond market, and officials describe it as emergency relief rather than a bailout that covers institutions&\#x27; operating risks. Participating institutions must continuously meet financial-market prudential requirements and maintain market discipline, and the support is provided through central bank bill borrowing rather than direct cash injection.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 26, 15:20

**Background**: China&\#x27;s wealth management subsidiaries and public funds are major holders of bonds but, unlike banks, do not have routine access to PBOC liquidity facilities such as open market operations or the standing lending facility. During past redemption waves, such as the late-2022 wealth management selloff, funds under redemption pressure sold bonds into falling markets, deepening price declines and triggering further redemptions. The PBOC already has a non-bank swap arrangement aimed mainly at the stock market, but BNSF is tailored to the bond market and to large-redemption stress at asset managers.
