## [Apple ships iOS 27 with rebuilt Siri AI and speedups for older iPhones](https://www.aibase.com/news/31048) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Apple has officially released iOS 27, a major update that keeps the Liquid Glass design language, rebuilds Siri with AI capabilities such as personal-context understanding, world knowledge, screen perception and system-level app operation, and refreshes Photos and Messages. The release also adds new AI features and parental controls, and optimizes performance for older devices, with support for iPhone 11 and later as well as the iPhone SE 2. Turning Siri from a voice-command assistant into a screen-aware, app-operating agent is Apple&\#x27;s most direct answer to the wave of AI assistants from Google, OpenAI and others, and it reaches an installed base of hundreds of millions of iPhones. At the same time, gating the most advanced features behind the newest chips creates a hardware upgrade pressure that will shape upgrade cycles and developer adoption of the new Siri APIs. The AI capabilities are hardware-gated: the redesigned Siri requires at least an iPhone 15 Pro, while some features are said to need an iPhone 17 Pro or later. The English beta ships alongside the system update, with French, Japanese and Korean to be added next month.

## [StepFun Releases StepAudio3 Series: Five Audio AI Models at Once](https://www.aibase.com/news/31063) ⭐️ 7.0/10

StepFun launched the StepAudio3 series on its open platform, releasing five audio models simultaneously: Realtime, ASR, TTS, Gen, and Music. The company claims several of them rank at the top of Artificial Analysis&\#x27;s global leaderboards. Rather than shipping a single speech model, StepFun has bundled an entire audio stack — recognition, synthesis, generation, real-time interaction, and music — into one coordinated release, signaling that Chinese labs are now competing across the whole voice pipeline, not just one link in it. For developers building voice agents, this adds a consolidated alternative to piecing together separate ASR, TTS, and dialogue vendors. The Realtime model is described as supporting native full-duplex dialogue, meaning it can listen and speak at the same time while picking up semantics, tone, and emotion; the line-up also covers complex speech understanding, human-level speech generation, full audio generation, and music creation. However, the announcement itself is brief and offers no benchmark numbers, model sizes, licensing terms, or independent validation of the Artificial Analysis ranking claims.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 15, 17:01

**Background**: Automatic speech recognition \(ASR\) converts spoken audio into text, while text-to-speech \(TTS\) synthesis does the reverse, turning written text into artificial speech judged by how human-like and intelligible it sounds. Full-duplex dialogue is a harder step up: conventional voice assistants alternate between listening and speaking in turns, whereas full-duplex systems process input and output audio streams simultaneously, letting users interrupt and be interrupted naturally. Artificial Analysis is a third-party evaluator that publishes comparative leaderboards for AI models, so a top ranking there is commonly used as a marketing claim of competitive performance.

## [Shanghai AI Lab Open-Sources Scholar-S2 Multimodal Model With Pluggable Memory Decoder](https://www.aibase.com/news/31056) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Shanghai AI Lab has open-sourced Scholar-S2, a multimodal foundation model whose general capabilities rank among the top tier of open-source models and which reportedly matches leading closed-source models on long-horizon scientific tasks in biology, materials, and chemistry. Its distinguishing feature is a Memory Decoder architecture with pluggable domain memory modules that act like an external brain, enabling deep domain specialization on demand without retraining the base model. The release pushes open-source multimodal models closer to closed-source frontier performance on scientific work, where long multi-step reasoning over experiments, materials, and molecules has been a key differentiator for proprietary systems. The plug-and-play memory design, if it holds up, could let researchers add specialized domain knowledge cheaply instead of fine-tuning or retraining billion-parameter models, lowering the barrier for AI-driven scientific discovery. The Memory Decoder concept, described in an August 2025 arXiv paper, uses a small transformer decoder pretrained to imitate an external non-parametric retriever, so domain memory can be swapped in as a module at inference time. The announcement itself is a brief blurb: it gives no benchmark numbers, no model size or parameter count, no licensing details, and the model name appears inconsistently as both &quot;Shueng-S2&quot; and &quot;Scholar-S2&quot;.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 15, 15:01

**Background**: Shanghai AI Laboratory is a major Chinese government-backed research institute that has previously released open scientific models such as Intern-S1, a scientific multimodal foundation model from the InternLM family. &quot;Long-horizon&quot; tasks refer to problems requiring many chained steps rather than a single answer, which are common in scientific workflows like planning experiments or reasoning across instrument outputs. Retrieval-augmented and memory-based approaches are a growing alternative to fine-tuning, because they let a frozen base model borrow external knowledge without degrading its general abilities.

Its worst single run still beat the best run of Claude Fable 5.1, and Astra also drove procurement costs down to roughly 48% of the original price. Vending-Bench2 is designed to measure whether an AI agent can stay coherent and profitable over a very long horizon, so Astra&\#x27;s roughly three-fold lead over Claude Fable 5.1 is a concrete signal that long-horizon agentic planning is improving quickly. If such gains hold, they point toward AI agents that can autonomously run ongoing business operations rather than just short, single-turn tasks. The task gives each model a $500 starting budget and scores it on the bank account balance after one simulated year of managing inventory, orders and pricing. The improvement is not only in profit but in strategy: Astra kept renegotiating supply costs, at one point cutting procurement expenses to about 48% of the original price, though the available report is brief and omits technical specifics such as run counts and variance.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 15, 10:01

**Background**: Vending-Bench2 is a benchmark from Andon Labs, following the original Vending-Bench, that tests an AI model&\#x27;s ability to run a simulated vending machine business autonomously over a full simulated year.
