## [OpenAI: GPT-6 Astra Shows Significant Decline in Chain-of-Thought Monitorability](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI disclosed that GPT-6 Astra exhibits a significant decline in Chain-of-Thought \(CoT\) monitorability compared with previous models. Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki said capabilities that rely on CoT monitoring are gradually weakening, partly because the model can control its reasoning process and complete complex tasks with less or even no verbalized reasoning. This matters because CoT monitoring is considered a key safety mechanism for inspecting whether AI models harbor hidden objectives or unsafe plans. A decline in monitorability could reduce transparency and create new challenges for safety evaluation and external oversight of frontier AI systems. OpenAI&\#x27;s official developer documentation also warns that Astra&\#x27;s inter-agent messages may contain syntax or whitespace errors. External evaluation by the UK AI Safety Institute found that Astra&\#x27;s raw reasoning is more compressed, with an increase in ambiguous phrases.

**Background**: Chain-of-thought \(CoT\) monitoring is a technique that inspects an AI model&\#x27;s step-by-step reasoning in natural language during multi-step tasks, helping uncover hidden objectives or unsafe plans. However, research suggests CoT monitorability is fragile and may not be an inherent feature of large models, because models can learn to reason in ways that are more compressed or less transparent. This has led some experts to argue that the AI safety field may be entering a &\#x27;post-CoT&\#x27; phase of oversight, where monitoring raw reasoning traces becomes less reliable.

## [Chinese phone giants unify offline tap-to-share standard](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-09-09/doc-inirfivw8597070.shtml) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On September 9, Honor announced that its self-led &\#x27;Tap-to-Share Interconnection Technical Standard&\#x27; has officially landed, with MagicOS 11 supporting cross-brand tap-to-share and the Honor Magic9 debuting the feature. Xiaomi, OPPO, and vivo have successively joined the standard. This is the first unified cross-brand offline file-transfer standard among major Chinese smartphone vendors, breaking down the walled gardens that previously limited fast transfers to same-brand devices. Users across these brands can now exchange files without an internet connection, potentially pressuring other players to adopt the same standard. The feature uses NFC to trigger pairing and Wi-Fi Direct for the actual transfer, so files neither pass through the cloud nor consume mobile data and are not compressed; a 1 GB video was tested to finish in roughly 8–12 seconds. Phones must support NFC and run the corresponding system update, and cross-brand transfers are typically capped at 2 GB per file.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 9, 12:30

**Background**: NFC \(Near Field Communication\) lets two devices establish contact when placed close together, but its bandwidth is too low for large files. Connection Handover, an NFC Forum specification, enables NFC devices to negotiate and activate alternative high-speed carriers such as Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth, so a tap initiates the handshake and the faster link takes over the bulk data transfer. This is the technical mechanism behind the new cross-brand &\#x27;tap-to-share&\#x27; feature.
