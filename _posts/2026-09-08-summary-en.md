## [ASML and TSMC Push High-NA EUV to 12-Inch Photomasks](https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2026/09/08/asml-gaat-samenwerken-met-taiwanese-chipgigant-tsmc-om-zijn-nieuwste-chipmachines-te-upgraden-a4936073) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On September 7, 2026, ASML and TSMC announced an industry partnership to move High-NA EUV lithography from the traditional 6-inch photomask to a larger 12-inch \(6×12-inch\) mask format. Roadmap targets include a 12-inch mask pilot production line by 2031 and the use of these systems in advanced-node high-volume manufacturing by 2033, with TSMC planning High-NA insertion from 2030. The larger mask format is projected to increase scanner productivity by roughly 40% and to eliminate much of the throughput loss that has made High-NA EUV extremely expensive. If successful, it would lower per-chip costs for advanced AI and logic chips, strengthening the business case for ASML’s next-generation machines at TSMC, Intel and Samsung. The new masks will measure roughly 6 by 12 inches instead of the industry-standard 6-inch square format, and the work is part of a &\#x27;Large Mask Consortium&\#x27; that reportedly includes TSMC, Samsung and Intel. The mask-size change directly targets the throughput constraints of ASML&\#x27;s High-NA EUV platform \(such as the EXE:5000 series\), where smaller mask fields would otherwise force multiple exposures or stitching for large logic chips.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 8, 06:55

**Background**: High-NA EUV is ASML&\#x27;s next-generation extreme ultraviolet lithography platform, with the first system delivered in December 2023 and high-volume manufacturing targeted for 2025–2026. In lithography, a photomask \(reticle\) is the template whose circuit pattern is projected onto silicon wafers. High-NA EUV enables smaller features with single-exposure patterning, but its smaller imaging field reduces throughput and creates stitching constraints. Enlarging the photomask from the current 6-inch square to a 6×12-inch format is therefore seen by ASML and chipmakers as a way to keep the resolution benefit while improving productivity and lowering cost.

## [Teen reading scores hit two-decade low as screen time surges: OECD](https://www.ithome.com/0/999/905.htm) ⭐️ 6.0/10

OECD&\#x27;s 2025 PISA tests of 760,000 15-year-olds across 91 countries show reading, math, and science scores at their lowest since 2000, with average reading scores dropping from 501 in 2012 to 466. The report ties rising screen entertainment and declining leisure reading to poorer results. These results offer fresh, large-scale evidence that heavy recreational screen time is associated with lower adolescent academic performance, which is significant as digital device use keeps rising among teens. The findings will inform debates for educators, parents, and policymakers about balancing technology use and reading habits. Reading scores fell from 501 in 2012 to 466, and OECD-member students averaged 3.4 hours per weekday on digital devices for entertainment. The report notes that scores began to decline once device use exceeded five hours per day.
