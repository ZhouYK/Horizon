## [FT: Prince Group associates accused of smuggling Nvidia chips into China](https://www.ft.com/content/f3ad8a81-3890-4a8a-8853-ff0a94b08ba9?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Financial Times reported that Hu Xiaowei, whom the U.S. Treasury has described as the &quot;number two&quot; figure in Cambodia&\#x27;s Prince Group, is alleged to have helped move Nvidia chips into China through a network of shell companies, underground financing channels and private jets. The report cites trade data showing that Megaspeed&\#x27;s Malaysian subsidiary declared roughly $4.6 billion in imports between December 2024 and December 2025, with some of those chips said to have ended up in China&\#x27;s black market. Prince Group denied that Hu ever worked for it and said the allegations about an illegal network and private-jet smuggling lacked factual basis. The case illustrates how large-scale circumvention of U.S. export controls may be operating through Southeast Asian intermediaries, putting billions of dollars of advanced AI hardware outside the intended licensing regime. It also widens the compliance risk for data-center operators, cloud resellers and logistics firms across the region, and raises pressure on Washington and Kuala Lumpur to tighten enforcement. The $4.6 billion figure reflects declared import values for Megaspeed&\#x27;s Malaysian unit rather than confirmed chip deliveries to China, and Prince Group is already under U.S. Treasury sanctions, meaning any transaction with its network carries legal exposure. Nvidia has repeatedly said it complies with export rules and that it cannot track hardware once it is sold to downstream buyers.

## [Tesla Begins Rolling Out FSD v15 With New Reality View](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2124222447855743019) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Tesla has started rolling out FSD v15 in overseas markets, claiming a 25x improvement in handling safety-critical cases and upgrading every part of the end-to-end driving network. The update also introduces &quot;Tesla Reality View,&quot; a real-time generative visualization that overlays a rendered view of the car&\#x27;s surroundings and FSD&\#x27;s planned path onto the in-car screen, with full-screen, map, and split-screen modes plus a quiet mode. This is one of Tesla&\#x27;s most visible FSD releases in a while: the 25x safety claim, if validated, targets exactly the rare edge cases that have kept supervised self-driving from wider acceptance. Reality View also turns opaque end-to-end AI decisions into something passengers can watch and understand, which could shape how users trust — and regulators scrutinize — the system. The 25x figure is a Tesla marketing claim with no published methodology, baseline, or third-party verification behind it, and the release notes describe only a general &quot;upgrade every part of the end-to-end driving network.&quot; Beyond the visualization, v15 tunes comfort details such as softer, fading turn-signal chimes, better handling of potholes and debris, and speed control near schools and in bad weather.

**Background**: FSD \(Full Self-Driving\) is Tesla&\#x27;s driver-assistance software, which despite the name still requires an attentive driver ready to take over. In its current &quot;end-to-end&quot; architecture, camera input goes into a single neural network that directly outputs steering, acceleration, and braking decisions, learned largely from footage collected by Tesla&\#x27;s customer fleet — which makes the system powerful but its reasoning hard to inspect. Reality View is meant to address that opacity by rendering a movable, game-like third-person view from a virtual camera above the car, showing what the network is planning. Tesla has been rolling FSD out region by region, so a release arriving &quot;overseas&quot; first means availability still varies significantly by market.

## [Phison launches space-grade SSD line, 488 TB headed to orbit in 2027](https://videocardz.com/newz/phison-announces-488tb-of-ssd-storage-for-space-data-centers-pricing-expected-to-be-astronomical) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Phison announced its Pascari Space SSD family, aimed at spacecraft, satellites and orbital data centers, and confirmed the line will be used aboard Lonestar Data Holdings&\#x27; StarVault-001 orbital data center. That mission is planned to launch in the first half of 2027 carrying 488 TB of Phison SSD storage, with roughly 370 TB usable once in low Earth orbit. It marks one of the first concrete, scheduled deployments of high-capacity commercial SSDs in orbit, signaling that the nascent orbital data center market is moving from concept to hardware contracts. If it works, it could open a new storage segment built around disaster recovery, sovereign data hosting and edge processing outside Earth&\#x27;s physical and geopolitical risk surface. The family is split into Flight Critical, Main Storage and Orbital Data Center tiers, with drives topping out at 128 TB, and Phison says pSLC mode can extend write endurance by up to 60 times on some models. The 488 TB figure is total vehicle capacity rather than a single drive, and the roughly 370 TB usable reflects redundancy and over-provisioning overhead; the products are already on sale.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 22, 10:31

**Background**: Lonestar Data Holdings is building what it calls sovereign, secure orbital data centers, selling services such as StarVault storage and disaster recovery from space; StarVault-001 is the hardware platform for that plan. pSLC is a technique that writes a single bit per cell on MLC or TLC NAND, trading capacity for far higher endurance and write performance — a sensible trade-off for drives that cannot be easily serviced once launched. Space SSDs must additionally cope with radiation, vacuum and thermal extremes that terrestrial enterprise drives never face.
