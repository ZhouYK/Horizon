On August 31, the European Commission designated ChatGPT as a very large online search engine and Reddit and Roblox as very large online platforms under the Digital Services Act \(DSA\), because each exceeds 45 million monthly active users in the EU. The three services now have a four-month transition period before stricter obligations take effect. This designation subjects two major social/community services and, notably, an AI chatbot to the EU&\#x27;s toughest digital safety rules, signaling that AI and gaming platforms are now firmly inside the scope of European platform regulation. It could force significant changes in content moderation, risk assessment, and transparency practices that affect hundreds of millions of users. During the four-month transition, the companies must conduct annual systemic risk assessments, submit to independent audits, and share data with regulators and vetted researchers on issues including illegal content, protection of minors, and users&\#x27; mental and physical well-being. ChatGPT&\#x27;s classification as a very large online search engine is notable because it applies search-related obligations to an AI conversational service.

**Background**: The EU Digital Services Act \(DSA\) is a landmark regulation that sets clearer accountability rules for online intermediaries and platforms in the European Union. Platforms with more than 45 million average monthly users in the EU are designated as very large online platforms \(VLOPs\) or very large online search engines \(VLOSEs\) and face stricter duties to assess and mitigate systemic risks such as disinformation, illegal content, and harms to minors. The DSA came into force in 2023-2024, and this new designation extends its most rigorous tier to AI chatbots and game-centric platforms for the first time. ChatGPT is a generative AI assistant, Reddit is a discussion forum, and Roblox is a user-generated gaming platform, all of which have substantial EU user bases.

## [OpenAI Codex tests new context management that switches windows instead of summarizing](https://github.com/openai/codex/pull/27488) ⭐️ 6.0/10

OpenAI is testing a new context window management approach for Codex that, instead of summarizing history when the limit is reached, opens a fresh window and lets the model proactively request a switch. The change is still in development across GitHub PRs \#27488, \#29743, and \#39827. Summarization-based compression both consumes extra tokens and risks losing important details, so a switch-to-new-window model could make long-running coding sessions more reliable and less costly. If it ships, it will affect all Codex users and may influence how other agentic coding tools handle context limits. Under the new scheme, model-initiated window switches, manual cleanup, and automatic cleanup all funnel into the same new-window flow, and no summary is generated. Accompanying history and notes features let the model retrieve previous content on demand after switching windows so work can continue without interruption.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 31, 00:02

**Background**: A context window is the amount of text a large language model can hold in memory while generating a response; once it fills up, the model either truncates or compresses older content. The most common compression method is summarization, where a shorter summary of the past conversation is kept in the context to preserve key decisions while freeing space. Codex is OpenAI&\#x27;s agentic coding tool that works inside a terminal, and this PR explores an alternative to summarization by switching to a new window and relying on external history and notes for continuity.

## [Cold Sequence Tech unveils MRAM inference roadmap: 24 TB/s uHBM, 2000+ tokens/s uLPU](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/adyFanNueXUHKnxr9m64kg) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Chinese startup Cold Sequence Technology \(寒序科技\), the country&\#x27;s first MRAM-based magnetic computing company, published its uHBM and uLPU inference computing architecture. The first-generation uHBM targets 24 TB/s on-chip read bandwidth, while uLPU aims to achieve over 2000 Tokens/s decode speed for 4B-parameter multimodal models, with a product roadmap spanning from chip to 2U tray and rack. This announcement highlights a novel approach to tackling the memory bandwidth bottleneck in AI inference by keeping model weights resident in persistent MRAM arrays, potentially reducing the energy and latency costs of repeatedly moving weights from external memory. If successful, it could offer an alternative to conventional HBM-based accelerators and influence how future inference hardware is designed. The architecture performs matrix-vector multiplication on-chip with weights stored in Persistent MRAM, avoiding repeated weight movement. Cold Sequence also disclosed that its SpinPU-ED01 validation chip has passed third-party testing and a 24-hour stability run, lending some credibility to the roadmap.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 31, 13:41

**Background**: AI inference is often constrained by memory bandwidth, as large language and multimodal models require frequent access to billions of weights. High Bandwidth Memory \(HBM\) is a 3D-stacked DRAM interface widely used in AI accelerators to provide high throughput. MRAM \(Magnetoresistive Random Access Memory\) is a non-volatile memory technology based on electron spin, offering a combination of speed, endurance, and persistence, which can potentially serve as a weight-stationary memory for inference chips.
