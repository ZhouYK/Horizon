## [Sanan Optoelectronics Controller Lin Xiucheng Formally Arrested on Embezzlement Charges](https://finance.caixin.com/2026-10-24/102496966.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Sanan Optoelectronics disclosed on October 24, 2026 that its actual controller Lin Xiucheng has been formally approved for arrest by prosecutors on suspicion of occupational embezzlement and misappropriation of company funds. The move follows roughly seven months of state-supervision detention \(留置\) that began in March 2026. Criminal charges against the controlling shareholder of a major A-share LED and compound semiconductor company represent a serious corporate-governance crisis and a market-moving risk event. The case could spill over to Sanan&\#x27;s Chongqing and Changsha semiconductor projects, which had received local government backing, and it leaves leadership uncertain at a strategically important domestic chip materials supplier. Sanan said vice chairman and general manager Lin Kechuang, Lin Xiucheng&\#x27;s son-in-law, had his detention lifted on October 8, 2026, after being placed under detention in April by the Yuzhong District Supervisory Commission in Chongqing. The two charges differ in intent: occupational embezzlement involves permanently appropriating company assets, while fund misappropriation typically involves temporary personal use or lending of company funds.

**Background**: Under China&\#x27;s supervisory system, 留置 is a detention measure used by supervisory commissions in corruption and duty-related investigations; it is distinct from criminal detention by police and can be converted into criminal proceedings. After criminal detention, prosecutors must review and decide whether to approve arrest \(批准逮捕\), a step that signals the case is moving forward as a criminal prosecution. Occupational embezzlement and misappropriation of funds are separate offenses under China&\#x27;s Criminal Law, distinguished mainly by whether the intent was to permanently take company assets or only to use them temporarily.

## [Executives quit $25bn Alaska LNG venture over alleged secret Russian control](https://www.ft.com/content/e9cfbfbb-e65a-4303-aebb-20d59319ef1b?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Executives at the $25bn Polar Train LNG project in Alaska resigned after the venture&\#x27;s former CEO told the Financial Times that Russia&\#x27;s Novatek secretly financed and controlled the strategic project without approval from Washington. The claim alleges that the Russian gas giant held the reins of an Arctic-linked US export scheme while remaining out of public view. The allegations raise serious national-security and sanctions questions, because a US LNG export project tied to stranded North Slope gas would be effectively controlled by a company subject to Western energy sanctions. If substantiated, the case could complicate US LNG permitting, deter investors and partners, and sharpen scrutiny of foreign ownership in strategic energy infrastructure. The project, led until now by CEO Joel Riddle, is designed to tap stranded Alaskan North Slope gas, pipe it a short distance offshore and liquefy it at a nearshore gravity-based structure before loading it onto ice-class LNG carriers for Asian markets. Novatek is Russia&\#x27;s second-largest natural gas producer after Gazprom and has itself been targeted by Western sanctions, making any undisclosed ownership role a potential legal and compliance problem.

rss · FT Markets · Oct 24, 00:22

**Background**: LNG \(liquefied natural gas\) is natural gas cooled to about −162°C so it becomes liquid and can be shipped by tanker, which is how gas from remote regions reaches overseas buyers. Alaska&\#x27;s North Slope holds enormous gas reserves that have stayed largely undeveloped because there was no economical way to move them to market, and the Polar Train/Polar LNG proposal is one attempt to solve that by building liquefaction capacity near the coast. Novatek is a Russian gas producer whose Arctic LNG projects have been constrained by sanctions, so its alleged undisclosed role in a US project raises red flags for regulators.

## [U.S. Treasury Moves to Restrict Biotech Investment in China](https://www.caixin.com/2026-10-24/102496975.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On October 22, 2026, the U.S. Treasury Department issued a proposed rule implementing the COINS Act and updating the Outbound Investment Security Program \(OISP\), adding biotechnology for the first time to the sensitive technology areas subject to outbound investment restrictions on China. The proposal could cover NewCo, Co-Co and equity investment structures, while license-out deals and CRO outsourced research are explicitly said to be unaffected. This expands U.S. national-security screening from semiconductors, AI and quantum computing into biotechnology, potentially chilling cross-border BD transactions and Chinese biotech companies&\#x27; access to U.S. capital through NewCo structures. The impact is tempered because license-out and CRO outsourcing remain outside the scope and the rule is still a draft. The COINS Act was enacted in December 2025 as part of the FY2026 National Defense Authorization Act and initially listed five sensitive areas: semiconductors and microelectronics, AI systems, quantum information technology, high-performance and supercomputing, and hypersonic systems. Treasury has authority to add technology areas, and the article names House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party chair John Moolenaar as a key advocate for adding biotechnology; the proposed rule is not yet final and remains subject to comment.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 24, 15:05

**Background**: The COINS Act is an outbound investment screening law that authorizes the U.S. Treasury to prohibit or require notification of American investments in sensitive technologies in countries of concern, and the OISP is the program that implements these controls. In Chinese biotech, the NewCo model involves licensing a drug asset&\#x27;s overseas rights to a newly formed offshore company backed by foreign capital and an international management team, while license-out is a direct licensing deal typically paying upfront, milestone and royalty fees. CROs such as WuXi AppTec provide outsourced research and development services to global pharmaceutical companies.

## [Cboe Captures Nearly Half of US ETF Listings, Challenging NYSE and Nasdaq](https://www.ft.com/content/8dd8e115-c45e-4b66-8390-b5eeea4671d5?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Cboe Global Markets has captured nearly half of new US ETF listings, positioning itself as a serious third competitor to the long-dominant NYSE and Nasdaq in the ETF listing business. The shift comes as issuers rush to launch derivative-heavy funds, a product category where Cboe&\#x27;s options-market heritage gives it an edge. ETF listing is a lucrative, high-volume franchise that has historically been a duopoly, so a credible third venue changes the bargaining dynamics for issuers seeking lower fees and better market-making support. It also signals how the ETF industry&\#x27;s center of gravity is shifting toward complex, options-based products rather than plain vanilla index trackers. The competitive pressure is concentrated in derivative-heavy strategies such as covered-call, buffer and options-income ETFs, which require exchanges with strong options infrastructure and specialized surveillance. Cboe&\#x27;s gains reflect new listings rather than a wholesale migration, meaning NYSE and Nasdaq still hold the vast majority of the existing installed base of ETF assets.

rss · FT Markets · Oct 24, 04:00

**Background**: An ETF is a fund that trades on an exchange like a stock and typically tracks an index, sector, or basket of assets; before it can trade, the issuer must list it on a specific exchange, which then handles trading, surveillance and market-making arrangements. Cboe, originally the Chicago Board Options Exchange, built its reputation as the venue where standardized, exchange-traded options became widely available, and it has since expanded into a multi-asset exchange operator. Because options-based ETFs rely on derivatives, listing them on a venue with deep options expertise can simplify product design and regulatory review, giving Cboe a natural advantage in that fast-growing niche.

## [Kimi launches 3-trillion-parameter K3.1 Pro with 1M context](https://www.caixin.com/2026-10-24/102496831.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

On October 24, 2026, Moonshot AI \(Kimi\) released Kimi K3.1 Pro, its first Pro-tier model and the first model in the K3.1 series. The 3-trillion-parameter model supports a 1-million-token context, claims notable gains in coding, 3D creation, financial research and long-horizon agent tasks, and comes with lower pricing. The release raises the bar in China&\#x27;s LLM price-performance race, pressuring rivals that have recently pushed lower-cost Flash-level models. It also gives developers and enterprises a new high-end option for long-context and agentic workloads, even though Moonshot is privately held and the direct stock-market impact is limited. Kimi describes K3.1 Pro as a post-trained version of K3, with gains across domains from increased post-training compute and large-scale reinforcement learning. The report does not detail active parameter count, benchmark results or exact pricing, and the first use of a &\#x27;Pro&\#x27; label leaves room for cheaper models in the K3.1 line.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 24, 15:05

**Background**: Post-training is the stage after pretraining in which a base model is fine-tuned and optimized with methods such as supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to improve instruction following, reasoning and task performance. A 1-million-token context window means the model can process roughly 1 million tokens in a single request, which is useful for large codebases or long documents but does not guarantee that it will use every relevant detail. Long-horizon agent capabilities refer to an agent&\#x27;s ability to plan and execute multi-step tasks over extended interactions rather than answering a single prompt.

## [China&\#x27;s environment ministry issues 2026 climate report, advances 39-city climate-adaptive pilot](https://science.caixin.com/2026-10-24/102496927.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

At a State Council Information Office briefing held ahead of COP31, Vice Minister of Ecology and Environment Li Gao released the &quot;China&\#x27;s Policies and Actions for Addressing Climate Change 2026 Annual Report&quot; and pushed forward a 39-city pilot to deepen climate-adaptive city construction. The report states that non-fossil energy rose from 16.0% of total energy consumption in 2020 to 21.7% in 2025, renewables generated 3.99 trillion kWh or 38% of all electricity used, and solar PV overtook coal at end-July 2026 to become China&\#x27;s largest installed power source. The data mark a structural shift rather than a cyclical one: all of 2025&\#x27;s electricity demand growth was met by new renewables, meaning coal-fired generation is increasingly relegated to a balancing role even as absolute capacity stays large. For solar, wind, storage and coal-power investors, the report is an official confirmation of the direction of travel, and it sets the baseline China will present at COP31 in Antalya in November 2026. Thermal generation posted its first year-on-year decline in roughly a decade in 2025, and non-fossil energy has now surpassed oil to become China&\#x27;s second-largest energy source after coal, with renewables accounting for 2.455 billion kW, or 60.7%, of total installed capacity in the first half of 2026. One caveat: the 39-city shortlist itself was first published at the 2024 National Low Carbon Day event in Changzhou \(including Shenzhen, Haikou, Fuzhou, Changchun, Yinchuan and Xining\), so the 2026 report deepens and reiterates an existing pilot rather than naming a new one.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 24, 15:05

**Background**: COP31, the 31st Conference of the Parties to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change, is scheduled for November 9-20, 2026 in Antalya, Turkey, with adaptation finance and technology transfer among its core agenda items. China publishes an annual climate policy report in the run-up to each COP as an official account of its mitigation and adaptation efforts. &quot;Climate-adaptive cities&quot; are a Chinese planning framework that embeds climate-risk assessment, resilient infrastructure and extreme-weather response into urban planning, construction and management; the &quot;non-fossil energy share&quot; metric counts nuclear plus renewables against total primary energy consumption, which is why it is lower than the renewables share of electricity.
