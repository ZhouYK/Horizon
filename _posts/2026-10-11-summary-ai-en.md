Microsoft Chairman and CEO Satya Nadella publicly called for advanced AI systems to be built with containment, independent outside controls, and an &quot;emergency brake&quot; that lets humans pause or shut down frontier models mid-task, according to reports from around October 10-11, 2026. Microsoft President Brad Smith had already publicly backed the emergency-brake idea in September 2026, giving the proposal institutional weight beyond a single executive&\#x27;s remarks.
