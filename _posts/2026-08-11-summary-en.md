## [Anthropic to Add AI Marking to Claude Content](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Anthropic has signed the EU AI Act Article 50\(2\) code of conduct and announced that Claude models launched in the EU on or after August 2, 2026 will mark AI-generated content at launch. Generated text will carry embedded machine-readable watermarks, and supported files will include digitally signed C2PA provenance metadata, across Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and the API. This makes Claude one of the first major AI assistants to proactively embed provenance signals at scale in response to the EU AI Act. It gives users and regulators a practical way to verify whether content may have been AI-generated, and could push other providers toward similar transparency measures. The text watermark is invisible and designed to survive copy-pasting and some editing, but detection only indicates content may have been processed by Claude—absence of a watermark does not prove human authorship. Anthropic is also retrofitting older models released before August 2, 2026, and plans to publish technical details of the detection method.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 11, 03:06

**Background**: Article 50 of the EU AI Act establishes transparency obligations for AI providers, including making synthetic content detectable. C2PA is an open technical standard, backed by companies such as Adobe, Microsoft, and Google, that adds cryptographically signed metadata to media files to certify their origin and editing history; Anthropic is adopting this standard for supported file types.

## [Graphene-Powered Soft Lens Could Revolutionize Cameras and Medical Devices](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/news/latest-news/2026/science-and-engineering/se/new-graphene-powered-soft-lens-could-pave-the-way-for-smarter-glasses-cameras-and-medical-devices.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Researchers at Queen Mary University of London have demonstrated a soft lens made from reduced graphene oxide that changes its focal length when a small electric field is applied, eliminating the need for bulky mechanical parts. The prototype, published in \*Advanced Functional Materials\*, mimics the human eye by stretching a soft membrane to refocus. This could enable compact autofocus cameras, wearable displays, VR/AR headsets, and miniaturized medical imaging devices that are lighter and more energy-efficient than current systems. It represents a step toward replacing traditional lens assemblies that rely on moving parts with solid-state, electrically controlled optics. The team embedded ultra-thin transparent graphene electrodes directly into the actuator layer beneath the lens, overcoming the limitation of standard opaque electrodes that could only be placed at the lens edge. Further optimization of electrode transparency and performance is still needed before commercialization.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 11, 12:27

**Background**: Reduced graphene oxide is a chemically or thermally reduced form of graphene oxide that restores electrical conductivity while remaining processable in solution, making it attractive for flexible electronics and actuators. Electrically tunable lenses use actuators—often made of electroactive polymers or dielectric elastomers—to deform a transparent membrane and adjust optical power without mechanical movement. The new work applies these principles to a lens with integrated transparent electrodes, achieving a compact design reported in a peer-reviewed journal.
