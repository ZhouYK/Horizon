## [WeChat AI Open-Sources WeMM-Embedding Multimodal Embedding Models](https://www.aibase.com/news/30896) ⭐️ 8.0/10

WeChat AI has open-sourced WeMM-Embedding, a family of universal multimodal embedding models available in 2B, 4B, and 9B parameter versions, capable of handling text, images, video, visual documents, and arbitrary mixed multimodal inputs. The models are published on GitHub, with a detailed technical report available on arXiv. This open-source release gives developers and researchers access to a production-grade multimodal embedding model family, which can advance cross-modal retrieval and multimodal AI research. It also offers a powerful open alternative to proprietary embedding APIs, potentially reshaping the ecosystem for semantic search and retrieval. WeMM-Embedding incorporates a multimodal isomorphic core that aligns text and visual information, improving cross-modal retrieval. According to the technical report, the models achieved substantial gains on a 26-task in-house benchmark and consistent improvements across 14 online A/B tests at WeChat.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 8, 11:01

**Background**: Embedding models map data like text or images into vectors in a shared space, so similar items are located close together. Multimodal embedding models such as CLIP project different data types into the same vector space, enabling cross-modal tasks like image-to-text retrieval. WeMM-Embedding extends these capabilities to video, visual documents, and interleaved multimodal inputs, making it a more universal solution.
