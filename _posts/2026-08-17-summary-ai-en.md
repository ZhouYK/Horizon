## [Nvidia&\#x27;s Reported 150 Trillion Won Guarantee for OpenAI Data Center Sparks Circular-Financing Concerns](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10843317) ⭐️ 8.0/10

According to a media report, Nvidia is providing a 150 trillion won \(about $110 billion\) guarantee for OpenAI&\#x27;s data center project. The arrangement has sparked controversy over circular financing, because Nvidia both supplies chips to the project and backs its debt. This underscores how deeply AI infrastructure deals are intertwining chipmakers with their customers&\#x27; finances. If OpenAI&\#x27;s data-center debt faces stress, Nvidia could be exposed financially, tying the fate of the leading GPU maker to an AI lab&\#x27;s capital structure. The reported guarantee is tied to vendor finance, a structure where a seller supports the buyer&\#x27;s purchase of its own products. Critics warn this creates a circular relationship, since Nvidia&\#x27;s revenue relies on OpenAI&\#x27;s ability to fund and build the data center.

gdelt · biz.heraldcorp.com · Aug 17, 22:45

**Background**: Vendor finance is a form of lending in which a company, instead of a bank, lends or guarantees money to help a customer buy its products; it may also take shares in the borrower. In this case, a circular-financing concern arises when the chipmaker guarantees debt that will ultimately be used to purchase its own GPUs. Large AI data centers require enormous capital, making such vendor-backed arrangements more common as chip vendors and AI labs become mutually dependent.
