## [Anthropic&\#x27;s early greenshoe exercise questioned after post-IPO share weakness](https://www.ft.com/content/d501198c-14a4-4b96-8880-c3997545902e) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Anthropic&\#x27;s underwriters exercised the IPO&\#x27;s &quot;greenshoe&quot; over-allotment option, lifting total proceeds to $93.5 billion from 1,127,000,000 shares priced at $83 each. The Financial Times reports that subsequent share-price weakness has raised questions about whether the banks exercised that option too hastily rather than holding it as a price-stabilization buffer. The greenshoe is the only price-stabilization mechanism explicitly permitted by the SEC in a US IPO, so exercising it early effectively spends the underwriters&\#x27; ammunition before it may be needed. For a marquee AI listing like Anthropic, the decision affects how the deal is read by investors, employees whose lock-up value depends on the share price, and other AI companies weighing their own mega-IPOs. A greenshoe allows underwriters to sell up to 15% more shares than planned within a roughly 30-day window \(capped at 15% under FINRA Rule 5110\), and banks normally choose between exercising it into strong demand and buying shares back in the market to support a weak price. The FT notes it seems likely that Anthropic approved, or even encouraged, the early exercise, though the article provides no specific price levels, named bankers, or detailed figures on the post-IPO decline.

rss · FT Markets · Nov 22, 11:00

**Background**: In a US IPO, underwriters typically sell about 15% more shares than the company originally planned, creating a short position that they must later cover. If the stock trades above the offer price, they cover by exercising the greenshoe — buying the extra shares from the company at the offer price and delivering them to investors. If the stock falls below the offer price, they instead buy shares in the open market, which both covers the short and props up the price; this is the only stabilization method the SEC explicitly permits. Exercising the option early therefore signals confidence in demand but gives up the ability to support the stock if it weakens during the stabilization window.
