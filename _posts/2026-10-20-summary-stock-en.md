Yttrium is an indispensable input for LED phosphors, lasers, medical radiotherapy and defence electronics, so a Japanese shortfall could ripple through global electronics, automotive and aerospace supply chains. rss · FT Markets · Oct 20, 04:03

**Background**: Rare earths are a group of 17 chemically similar metals \(the 15 lanthanides plus scandium and yttrium\) that are essential to magnets, phosphors, catalysts and electronics. China dominates global refining and separation capacity, giving it outsized leverage over downstream manufacturers even when other countries mine the ore. Yttrium, atomic number 39, is mostly used in phosphors for LEDs and displays, in yttria-stabilised zirconia ceramics and solid oxide fuel cells, in Nd:YAG lasers, and in yttrium-90 microspheres for cancer radiotherapy.

## [Guangdong Spot Power Prices Hit 198% Premium, Retail Suppliers Face Huge Losses](https://weekly.caixin.com/2026-10-16/102493974.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On October 13, 2026, Guangdong&\#x27;s electricity spot market price surged to a peak of 1.11 yuan/kWh during the evening peak, with a daily average of 0.808 yuan/kWh — respectively 198% and 117% above the average price of Guangdong&\#x27;s mid- and long-term power contracts. The rally has persisted since April, driven by soaring coal and natural gas costs and amplified by the fact that the five southern provinces \(Guangdong, Guangxi, Yunnan, Guizhou, Hainan\) now clear a shared regional spot market, causing prices to move in lockstep. Retail electricity suppliers sit at the end of the power supply chain: they buy in the spot market while selling to end users at previously agreed fixed prices, so a spot-contract spread of this magnitude can wipe out their margins and even their capital. The losses test the credibility of China&\#x27;s electricity market reform and raise questions about how price risk should be allocated among generators, retailers, and consumers, which matters for the entire power sector and for regulators designing the market rules. In China&\#x27;s spot market design, mid- and long-term contracts are typically settled as financial contracts for differences \(差价合约/差价结算\): the contract fixes the price the retailer pays, but the retailer still bears the spot-market price difference, so a 198% spike flows straight into its losses. The contagion is regional rather than local, because the southern regional market clears five provinces together, meaning cost shocks in one province quickly propagate to the others.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 20, 13:23

**Background**: China launched a new round of power sector reform in 2015, gradually replacing administratively set tariffs with market trading, and spot markets became the core of that effort. A 2023 notice from the National Development and Reform Commission and the National Energy Administration \(Document No. 136\) accelerated spot market construction nationwide and clarified how contracts for differences and difference settlement would work. Retail electricity companies have been licensed in Guangdong since 2016 and multiplied across the country as more users were allowed to buy power through them; they profit on the spread between wholesale purchases and retail sales, a model that is highly vulnerable when spot prices spike.
