## [India&\#x27;s central bank intervenes as rupee nears record lows](https://www.ft.com/content/b373cf00-9a94-4dc7-bdda-bc7006da1148?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Reserve Bank of India is intervening in the foreign exchange market to support the rupee as the currency approaches record lows, with pressure attributed to higher energy costs stemming from the US-Israel conflict with Iran. The FT reports the currency weakness is hitting the world&\#x27;s fastest-growing major economy, and notes the central bank had previously raised $143.6bn from the Indian diaspora through schemes designed to attract dollars back into the country. This is a material macro and emerging-market development with spillovers to Indian equities, bonds and other emerging-market currencies, since a weakening rupee raises import costs and can fuel inflation. It also shows how a geopolitical shock in the Middle East can transmit quickly through energy prices into the currency and monetary policy of a large oil-importing economy. The report does not disclose specific intervention amounts, nor any rate decision or change in the policy regime, which limits visibility into how aggressively the RBI is defending the currency. India is one of the world&\#x27;s largest crude oil importers, so its import bill and dollar demand move closely with global oil prices, and the RBI has already tapped diaspora deposits as a dollar-raising channel.

rss · FT Markets · Oct 10, 11:57

**Background**: Currency intervention occurs when a central bank buys or sells foreign currency against its own domestic currency in order to influence the exchange rate. To defend a falling currency, a central bank typically sells dollars from its foreign exchange reserves and buys its own currency, which drains liquidity and can be sterilized to limit the impact on the monetary base. India&\#x27;s rupee is especially sensitive to oil prices because crude is priced in dollars and India imports most of what it consumes, so rising energy costs translate directly into higher demand for dollars and downward pressure on the rupee.

## [US ITC Launches Section 337 Probe Into Vertical Power Delivery Systems](https://www.caixin.com/2026-10-10/102492142.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On October 9, 2026, the U.S. International Trade Commission voted to open a Section 337 investigation into certain vertical power delivery systems, their components, and downstream computing systems. The named respondents span China, the United States, Canada and Germany, and include Delta Electronics, Luxshare Precision \(and its Dongguan unit\), Chengdu Monolithic Power Systems, Quanta Computer, Hon Hai \(Foxconn\), Foxconn Industrial Internet and Ingrasys, as well as Infineon. If the ITC eventually finds infringement, it can issue exclusion orders or cease-and-desist orders that block the affected products from entering the United States, directly touching the AI server and data-center power supply chain where these companies are major suppliers. The case signals that trade friction between Washington and Asian electronics makers is spreading from chips and networking gear into AI compute infrastructure components. The action is only at the investigation-initiation stage, so no infringement finding, remedy or financial impact has been determined yet; Section 337 cases are typically driven by patent or other intellectual-property complaints and often take roughly 12 to 18 months to reach a final determination. The scope covers vertical power delivery systems and their components used in data-center servers and AI systems, plus the computing systems that contain them.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 10, 13:06

**Background**: Section 337 of the U.S. Tariff Act of 1930 empowers the USITC to investigate allegedly unfair import practices such as patent infringement, and its main remedies are exclusion orders and cease-and-desist orders rather than monetary damages. Vertical power delivery is a power architecture in which voltage regulation hardware sits directly beneath or beside the processor instead of elsewhere on the board, shortening the current path to cut resistive losses and improve efficiency for high-power AI chips. Companies like Delta, Luxshare, Quanta and Foxconn are key players in server power modules and AI server assembly, which is why a single ITC case can pull in a wide web of suppliers.

## [Standard Chartered Exec: RMB Internationalization Enters &quot;2.0 Phase&quot; as Commercial Value Accelerates](https://finance.caixin.com/2026-10-10/102492182.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Lu Jing, President and Vice Chair of Standard Chartered China, said in a Caixin interview that RMB internationalization has entered a &quot;2.0 phase,&quot; citing central bank data showing that over 50% of China&\#x27;s cross-border receipts and payments \(covering trade and cross-border investment\) are now settled in RMB. She noted that offshore RMB deposits in major markets stood at about RMB 1.8 trillion as of the end of Q1 2026, RMB 126.1 billion higher than at end-2025 and near a historic high, with Hong Kong alone holding RMB 1.0352 trillion. Crossing the 50% threshold in RMB-settled cross-border payments marks a structural shift in how global trade and investment flows are denominated, gradually reducing reliance on a single-currency system and expanding the addressable market for RMB-denominated assets and banking services. It matters for exporters and importers weighing financing costs, for offshore financial centers such as Hong Kong, and for banks positioning themselves around RMB clearing, FX and treasury products. Lu Jing pointed to the efficiency gains still to come from infrastructure including CIPS \(Cross-border Interbank Payment System\), mBridge, the digital RMB, and CBETS \(Cross-border e-CNY Transfer Services\), which she expects to further increase RMB use in cross-border settlement. She also framed cheaper financing costs as a key current driver for corporates to expand their use of RMB, while noting that international participants increasingly want diversified currency options rather than outright de-dollarization.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 10, 15:06

**Background**: CIPS is China&\#x27;s wholesale RMB cross-border clearing and settlement system, launched in 2015 as a dedicated RMB channel alongside the SWIFT network used for most cross-border dollar transactions. mBridge is a multi-central-bank digital currency platform jointly initiated in February 2021 by the BIS Innovation Hub Hong Kong, the Bank of Thailand, the Central Bank of the UAE, the PBoC&\#x27;s Digital Currency Institute and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority, built on distributed ledger technology and reaching minimum viable product status in mid-2024. CBETS, or &quot;数币达&quot;, is a state-level cross-border digital RMB clearing hub launched in 2026 by the Digital RMB International Operation Center in Shanghai, with the first batch of 26 domestic and overseas institutions signing on in June 2026. Offshore RMB deposit balances are a commonly watched gauge of how much RMB liquidity is held outside mainland China, with Hong Kong the dominant offshore center.

## [China Declares 2026/27 Super El Niño, Strongest Since Records Began](https://science.caixin.com/2026-10-10/102492120.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

On October 9, 2026, China&\#x27;s National Climate Center and the National Marine Environmental Forecasting Center announced that a super El Niño event had officially formed in September 2026, classified as the eastern-Pacific type with its warm core in the equatorial eastern Pacific. Forecasts indicate sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific will keep rising over the next three months and peak in late autumn to early winter, with peak intensity expected to reach 3.2–3.5°C. Because El Niño&\#x27;s effect on China&\#x27;s summer rainfall is strongly lagged, the climate center warns of a relatively high risk of severe flooding in the Yangtze River basin in 2027. A record-strength event of this kind has implications for agriculture, commodity markets, insurance and disaster preparedness both in China and globally. Compared with the three previous super El Niño events \(1982–1983, 1997–1998 and 2014–2016\), this one developed far faster, taking only 5 months from El Niño onset to super strength versus 7 to 15 months for the others. Its forecast peak of 3.2–3.5°C also exceeds the previous record peak of 2.8°C, making it the strongest event since China&\#x27;s systematic monitoring began in 1951.

rss · 财新网 · Oct 10, 13:06

**Background**: El Niño is a periodic warming of sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific that reshapes global weather patterns, and events whose peak anomalies exceed roughly 2.0°C are classed as &\#x27;super&\#x27; El Niño. Only three such events had been recorded before: 1982–1983, 1997–1998 and 2014–2016, all of which were associated with major climate disruptions. The Chinese government&\#x27;s National Climate Center maintains the country&\#x27;s official monitoring records for these events, which typically influence East Asian rainfall and typhoon activity with a delay of several months.
