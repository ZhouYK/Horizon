## [OpenAI Halts Astra Training as Model Nears Critical Cyberattack Threshold](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 18, 2026, OpenAI announced it paused reinforcement learning training of its upcoming Astra model for two weeks because the model may have reached its &quot;critical cybersecurity capability&quot; threshold, and its largest frontier RL run remains suspended. The company also added multi-stage automated investigations that aim to alert within 30 minutes of anomalies. This marks a significant shift in frontier AI development, treating advanced cyber capability as a deployment and safety-control question rather than only a content-moderation issue. It follows Anthropic&\#x27;s similar move, signaling a broader industry trend toward pausing training when models approach dangerous capability thresholds. According to OpenAI, the monitoring overhead accounts for about 20% of the monitored inference compute. The company has also strengthened alignment and safety protections alongside the expanded automated investigation systems.

**Background**: OpenAI&\#x27;s &quot;critical cybersecurity capability threshold&quot; is part of its Preparedness Framework, which defines levels of dangerous capability in domains such as cyber and triggers safety actions when a model approaches critical risk. Reinforcement learning \(RL\) training improves model performance through trial-and-error feedback, and pausing it allows time for additional evaluations and mitigations before deployment. Other organizations, such as Anthropic, have recently applied similar thresholds to their own models.

## [Moderna and Merck Report Phase 3 Success for Personalized mRNA Cancer Vaccine in Melanoma](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3779803) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 19, 2026, Moderna and Merck announced that their personalized mRNA cancer vaccine in combination with Keytruda met primary and key secondary endpoints in a Phase 3 adjuvant melanoma trial, significantly reducing recurrence and distant metastasis risk. The companies have not yet disclosed the exact magnitude of benefit. This is the first late-stage success of a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine, validating that &\#x27;one-person, one-shot&\#x27; precision immunotherapy can be scaled beyond a concept. It could reshape melanoma adjuvant treatment and pave the way for mRNA vaccines in other cancer types, with major implications for patients, biotech, and AI-guided personalized medicine. The trial will continue to assess overall survival, and the exact reduction in recurrence or metastasis risk has not been disclosed. Following the announcement, Moderna shares initially surged 90% in pre-market trading and later expanded gains to 150%, while Merck rose over 8%.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 19, 14:41

**Background**: Personalized mRNA cancer vaccines work by sequencing a patient&\#x27;s tumor to identify tumor-specific mutations, known as neoantigens, and then using mRNA to instruct cells to produce these neoantigens so the immune system can recognize and attack the cancer. Combining such vaccines with checkpoint inhibitors like Keytruda, a PD-1 blocker, aims to both generate a targeted immune response and remove brakes on T cells for a stronger, more durable antitumor effect. Prior to this Phase 3 result, the approach had shown promise in earlier trials but had not demonstrated definitive late-stage efficacy.

## [Apple Overhauls EU Fee Structure, Cutting Commissions for Alternative App Stores](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/apple-changes-fees-alternative-app-stores-eu-2026-08-18/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Apple announced new EU business terms effective October 1, replacing the per-install Core Technology Fee with a 5% Core Technology Commission on digital transactions for apps distributed outside the App Store. Commissions for apps using alternative payment processors in the App Store are set at 20%, reduced to 10% for small businesses. This is a significant regulatory-driven shift in how Apple monetizes iOS distribution in the EU, directly responding to the Digital Markets Act. Developers gain simpler, potentially lower costs, while Apple avoids per-install fees that drew criticism. The new model abolishes the previous initial acquisition fee and store services fee entirely. The 5% Core Technology Commission applies to digital goods and services sold in apps distributed outside the App Store, and the 20% alternative payment commission applies only within the App Store.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 19, 01:19

**Background**: The European Union&\#x27;s Digital Markets Act \(DMA\) designates Apple as a &\#x27;gatekeeper&\#x27; for iOS and the App Store, requiring it to allow alternative app marketplaces and payment systems. Apple initially introduced the Core Technology Fee \(€0.50 per install\) as part of new business terms, which drew developer criticism. The European Commission has welcomed the revised fee structure and said it will monitor compliance.
