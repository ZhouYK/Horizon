## [AMD to acquire World Labs for $8.2B; Fei-Fei Li joins as EVP](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1299/amd-to-acquire-world-labs-to-advance-the-future-of-ai-compute) ⭐️ 9.0/10

AMD announced an all-stock acquisition of World Labs, the world-model AI startup founded by Fei-Fei Li in 2024, in a deal valued at roughly $8.2 billion \(about RMB 55.1 billion\), with the announcement dated September 28. As part of the transaction, Li will join AMD as Executive Vice President and Chief Scientist, reporting directly to CEO Lisa Su, and the deal is expected to close by the end of the year subject to regulatory approval. This is one of the largest AI acquisitions by a chip vendor and signals that AMD intends to compete with NVIDIA not just on silicon but on the full stack of models and software for embodied AI and robotics. Bringing a researcher of Fei-Fei Li&\#x27;s stature in-house gives AMD a marquee AI brand and a direct path into robot simulation and physical-world AI, an area NVIDIA has staked out with Isaac Sim and Omniverse. World Labs was founded in 2024 and focuses on world models that let AI perceive, generate and reason about physical or virtual environments; its research includes generating spatially coherent environments for robot training simulated from real-world data. The reported structure is an all-stock transaction of about $8.2 billion, and the deal remains subject to regulatory approval, so the terms and timing could still change.

**Background**: A world model is a machine learning system that builds an internal representation of an environment and predicts how that environment changes in response to actions, which allows an agent to plan and reason without constant real-world trial and error. Unlike large language models that predict text, world models simulate dynamics such as physics, object interactions and causality, which makes them useful for robot training, autonomous driving and interactive video generation. AMD is the main challenger to NVIDIA in AI accelerators, with its Instinct data-center GPUs and ROCm software stack, but it has lagged in owning the models and developer ecosystem built on top of the hardware.

At its DevDay 2026 keynote on September 29 in San Francisco, OpenAI announced more than 20 updates, headlined by Dots, an always-on companion agent that learns user habits and takes over long-running work, and by two new models: GPT-6.1 Sol, which offers near-Astra intelligence at one-fifth of Astra&\#x27;s API token price, and Astra Ultrafast, which is up to 8x faster \(6x on the API\). The same keynote also introduced a Decisions API powered by GPT-6 Luna, an Agents API with native computer use and AWS Bedrock hosting, Codex on the cloud with voice control and automatic bug fixing, &quot;Sign in with ChatGPT&quot; for third-party tools, and a new Pro 500 subscription tier. The Decisions API is deliberately narrow — it uses GPT-6 Luna to classify text or image inputs into a finite set of user-defined answers for routing and agent action decisions — and early independent testing reported that Sol was the slowest of the compared models, with a rival model finishing the same task in about a sixth of the time at the same per-token price.

## [Oracle Issues Force Majeure Notice on Stargate&\#x27;s Project Jupiter Data Center](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/oracle-cites-force-majeure-to-shield-itself-on-controversial-data-center) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Oracle has issued a force majeure notice to the developers of Stargate&\#x27;s Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico, citing delays in environmental and power approvals for the site&\#x27;s 2.45GW microgrid. The notice is intended to defer some of Oracle&\#x27;s payments if external factors push back the project, which now risks missing its 2028 target in-service date. The move underscores that power supply and permitting, not chips or capital, are becoming the binding constraint on hyperscale AI data center buildouts. It also has concrete financial fallout: the project&\#x27;s $18 billion syndicated loan is reportedly trading at a discount, and Texas has already paused approvals for new data center projects. Project Jupiter&\#x27;s 2.45GW microgrid is the crux of the dispute, since a microgrid of that scale must secure both environmental clearance and grid interconnection approvals before it can energize the site. Most Stargate projects remain in civil construction, permitting, or energy-procurement stages, with only a small number of campuses such as the Abilene, Texas site actually operational.

**Background**: The Stargate Project is a joint venture announced in January 2025 by OpenAI, Oracle, SoftBank and others, with plans to invest up to $500 billion in U.S. AI infrastructure by 2029, starting with a flagship campus in Abilene, Texas. Project Jupiter is a roughly 1,400-acre AI data center development near Santa Teresa, New Mexico, and is one of the largest such projects under construction in the country. Because AI campuses consume enormous amounts of electricity, developers increasingly build on-site microgrids — localized power networks that can run alongside or independently of the main utility grid — rather than waiting years for utility interconnection. A force majeure clause lets a party suspend or defer contractual obligations when events outside its control, such as permitting delays, prevent performance.

## [Cloudflare launches cf CLI open beta for AI agents and developers](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-cf-cli-launch/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Cloudflare released the open beta of a new command-line tool called cf, which is generated from its API schema and exposes more than 3,000 Cloudflare API operations through the command line, compared with roughly 280 operations covered by the existing Wrangler CLI. The tool is aimed at both developers and AI agents, using JSON as its default output format and supporting command search and guided discovery so agents can find, execute, and parse operations on their own. This significantly widens what can be automated through Cloudflare&\#x27;s own tooling: instead of a narrow Workers-focused surface, agents and scripts get programmatic reach into nearly the entire Cloudflare product catalog. It reflects a broader industry shift toward making infrastructure APIs machine-readable and agent-friendly rather than purely human-oriented. Cloudflare highlights examples where a single tool lets an agent create and deploy a Worker, monitor services, configure Access and WAF, and even purchase a domain. The release is an open beta, so coverage, stability, and the exact command surface may still change; no community comments were available at the time of writing.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 29, 13:46

**Background**: Cloudflare Workers is Cloudflare&\#x27;s serverless platform that runs code across its global edge network, and Wrangler is the long-standing CLI used to build and deploy those Workers. Cloudflare Access is a Zero Trust Network Access component that enforces identity-based access to self-hosted and SaaS applications, while WAF is a web application firewall. The new cf CLI is instead generated directly from Cloudflare&\#x27;s API schema, which is why it can cover thousands of operations across many products rather than only the Workers workflow.

## [Google fixes Firebase bug that crashed iOS apps at launch](https://github.com/firebase/firebase-ios-sdk/issues/16728) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Google confirmed that malformed server-side data returned by Google Analytics for Firebase caused a large number of iOS apps that integrate the SDK to crash on launch. The incident began on September 28, 2026 at 17:41 PDT and was resolved by a server-side rollout completed at 19:52 PDT. The incident shows how fragile mobile apps become when a third-party SDK depends on remote server data: apps crashed without developers changing a single line of code, and there was nothing app teams could do locally to prevent it. For Firebase and mobile developers broadly, it underscores the operational risk of SDK-level remote configuration and the value of fast server-side rollbacks. Google stated that no SDK or app update was required, since the fix was delivered entirely on the server side; however, because of caching, some apps could continue to crash for up to roughly four hours after the fix, with the residual impact fading on its own. The notice was tracked in the firebase-ios-sdk GitHub repository under issue \#16728, and the total window from onset to fix was about two hours.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 29, 16:29

**Background**: Firebase is Google&\#x27;s platform for building mobile and web apps, offering services such as authentication, databases, and analytics; Google Analytics for Firebase is its analytics component, which app developers embed to collect usage events and app behavior data. Because such SDKs fetch configuration and data from Google&\#x27;s servers during app startup, a bad server response can break the app before any of the developer&\#x27;s own code runs. This is why a malformed payload from a single Google-side service can cause crashes across many unrelated apps at once.
