## [AI Decodes DNA Initiator Sequence in 60% of Human Genes](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMickFVX3lxTE1HdGxTRU41YThTUFJUT2xSYTBMdS1UdDNJMHNEbnFUUk1IaWZiZll6S2RPQktsM2cwVUd2cjJQQURZY1VrOVd6QjNVZlBUeTdDdmRaYTkzQzlQem56NDEzQmpCR1dsdkRuNnlIQTBIWTNKZw?oc=5) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers used AI to identify the DNA initiator element sequence in human genes, discovering that it appears in roughly 60% of all human genes. This marks a notable AI-driven advance in genomic research. Understanding the initiator sequence is key to deciphering gene regulation, since it helps determine where and when genes are transcribed. The finding could accelerate studies on gene expression, disease mechanisms, and personalized medicine, and highlights AI&\#x27;s growing role in bioinformatics. The initiator element \(Inr\) is a core promoter component with a consensus sequence of YYA+1NWYY in humans, capable of directing transcription without a TATA box. After decoding the motif, researchers scanned the genome and found it in about 60% of human genes.

**Background**: Gene expression begins at the promoter, a DNA region where transcription factors and RNA polymerase assemble. AI and machine learning are increasingly used to identify such regulatory patterns in large genomic data.
