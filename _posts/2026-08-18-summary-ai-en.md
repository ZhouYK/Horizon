## [Mojo Programming Language Goes Open Source Under Apache 2](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/18/mojo-is-now-open-source/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 18, 2026, Modular released the Mojo compiler and toolchain under the Apache 2 license, making the language fully open source. This follows the release of Mojo 1.0 the previous week and fulfills a promise made when the language was first introduced in May 2023. This marks a major milestone for Mojo, a high-profile programming language aimed at AI and machine learning, and opens the door for broader community adoption and contribution. It also strengthens Mojo&\#x27;s position in the developer ecosystem as an open, high-performance alternative to Python for GPU and accelerator programming. Mojo was originally designed as a superset of Python, but that goal was effectively abandoned in August 2025, and Mojo is now a separate language with Python-inspired syntax optimized for GPU programming, not fully compatible with existing Python code. The language is built on MLIR rather than directly on LLVM, which allows it to target GPUs, TPUs, and other accelerators.

## [SK Hynix and SanDisk Debut HBF Standard to Ease AI Memory Bottlenecks](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20260804000134) ⭐️ 8.0/10

SK Hynix and SanDisk unveiled the first High Bandwidth Flash \(HBF\) standard specifications at FMS 2026, with support from Google and Tenstorrent. This marks a key milestone in the global standardization of a new memory tier aimed at AI workloads. HBF is designed to fill the gap between HBM and SSDs, potentially alleviating the memory bandwidth and capacity bottlenecks that constrain large-scale AI inference and training. Standardizing HBF could reshape the AI memory hierarchy and benefit the broader AI hardware ecosystem by enabling interoperable, high-bandwidth flash solutions. HBF applies HBM-style stacking to NAND flash, positioning it as a middle layer between HBM and SSDs with throughput potentially reaching 3TB/s. SK Hynix has also proposed an HBM+HBF hybrid design, simulated with an Nvidia Blackwell GPU \(B200\) using 8 HBM3E stacks and 8 HBF stacks for LLM inference.

gdelt · newspim.com · Aug 18, 22:45

**Background**: In the AI era, memory systems traditionally follow a hierarchy from fast, small SRAM/DRAM through HBM down to large, slower SSDs. HBM offers high bandwidth but limited capacity and high cost, while SSDs provide storage density but insufficient speed for real-time AI model access. HBF, by stacking NAND flash in an HBM-like manner, aims to create a new tier with both high bandwidth and larger capacity. Standardization efforts like this one are crucial for enabling broad industry adoption and compatibility across vendors.

## [MIT Study: AI-Generated Images Often Untraceable to Training Data](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisAFBVV95cUxPZTZpTDNuME1qYU1VZkkySkRzSURWQTN0R1Z6REJDYmhSR3NrMVB2UEpDc0tLanFUM3JwX1c1eWNxWjBEZGs3M3djbDNzQlVtTW1BbWhYanhuN3Rob3AyVjluUTdTdFVkTWdNb3FwU1FSMmI0WE1wRjFtbnl1MWhXSVJBLXdrWVBxeW4zTDZzWjFsZG9ZejJQM2pIYkh2Rmp1X3EteFJkbDZobTVFMG94dA?oc=5) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers at MIT found that AI-generated images frequently cannot be traced back to the specific training data that influenced them. The study tested existing training data attribution methods and showed they often fail for generated images. This finding complicates efforts to assign authorship and enforce copyright in AI art, since creators cannot easily prove which original works shaped a generated image. It also raises questions about accountability for AI models that may reproduce or imitate copyrighted content without clear attribution. Training data attribution \(TDA\) methods, including influence functions, aim to estimate how removing certain data points would change a model&\#x27;s behavior. The MIT study indicates these methods are unreliable for complex generative image models, highlighting the need for better attribution techniques.

google\_news · MIT News · Aug 18, 16:35

**Background**: Training data attribution \(TDA\) is the process of identifying which specific training examples most influenced a model&\#x27;s output. Influence functions, a classic technique from robust statistics, are often used for this but only approximate local sensitivity around the final weights. Generative AI models learn from massive datasets, so pinning a single output to specific training images is a complex challenge. Without reliable tracing, legal claims about AI art authorship lack a strong technical foundation.

## [Harvard and MIT Launch MatrAIx, Simulating 8.3 Billion AI Agents](https://www.aibase.com/news/30440) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Harvard, MIT and partners have launched MatrAIx, a system that generates 8.3 billion AI agents simulating global human behavior across 1,290 dimensions. The system achieves 91.5% overall consistency with human responses and was validated by 400 controlled tests across 10 behavioral traits and 4 environments. This is a novel milestone in agent-based simulation, enabling low-cost, large-scale user testing that could transform social science research and AI evaluation. It may allow researchers to pre-test digital products or policies on a simulation of the entire global population before real-world deployment. The system covers 1,290 behavioral dimensions and achieves 91.5% consistency overall, but consistency varies by scenario across the 400 controlled tests. MatrAIx agents can fill surveys, chat, browse the web, and use apps, making it a &\#x27;simulated-user evaluation infrastructure&\#x27; for digital products and AI systems.

aibase · AIbase · Aug 18, 17:27

**Background**: Agent-based simulation is a computational modeling approach where individual &\#x27;agents&\#x27; with defined rules interact to observe emergent collective behavior. MatrAIx scales this to 8.3 billion persona agents to represent the global population, building on earlier work like one-billion-agent simulations and large-scale epidemiological models. The project aims to &\#x27;simulate before reality&\#x27; by allowing researchers to test systems against a realistic, heterogeneous user base before launching them.
