The upgraded pact has not yet been formally signed, and neither side has announced a signing date or an entry-into-force date, so the commitments remain political undertakings rather than binding obligations for now. Most-favoured-nation treatment only guarantees that Korean suppliers are treated no less favourably than suppliers from other foreign countries — it does not by itself remove content quotas, import approvals or licensing reviews, so actual market re-entry will depend on implementing rules.
