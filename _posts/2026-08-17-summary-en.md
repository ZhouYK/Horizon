## [Apple to Adjust App Ad Consent Rules After German Regulator Ruling](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/apple-change-app-data-consent-rules-german-regulator-says-2026-08-17/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Apple will change how app developers obtain permission to use personal data for targeted advertising on iPhone and iPad, following a German regulator ruling that its App Tracking Transparency \(ATT\) framework unfairly favored its own apps. The company must implement the changes within four months of the ruling and commit to them for seven years. The decision directly alters Apple&\#x27;s ATT framework, which has reshaped mobile advertising by letting users block IDFA tracking in iOS. It strengthens regulatory momentum against Apple across Europe, following €150 million and €98.6 million fines from France and Italy, and will affect app developers, advertisers, and user consent flows. The German ruling requires that third-party consent popups be neutral, without dissuasive wording or symbols intended to steer users away from allowing tracking. Apple must comply within four months after the order is served, and the commitment remains in force for seven years.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 17, 12:50

**Background**: App Tracking Transparency \(ATT\), introduced with iOS 14.5, requires apps to show a prompt and obtain user permission before accessing the Identifier for Advertisers \(IDFA\), a device identifier used for personalized ads and ad attribution. User opt-in rates have been low; Flurry Analytics reported that 96% of US users opted out of IDFA sharing in May 2021. Regulators in France and Italy previously fined Apple for ATT-related competition concerns, and the German decision adds further scrutiny to how Apple designs its own consent prompts.

GPT-5.6 Sol, released to general availability in July 2026, is OpenAI&\#x27;s flagship model with a 1.05M token native window, and enabling the full window in Codex requires explicitly setting these parameters.

OpenAI has added an opt-in Computer History feature to the ChatGPT macOS desktop app that records clicks and keystrokes to build an activity timeline. The timeline can be used to train ChatGPT and Codex, learn work habits, suggest automations, and take over tasks. The feature is off by default and lets users exclude specific apps and websites, delete records, and ignore incognito or private tabs. **Background**: ChatGPT is OpenAI&\#x27;s conversational AI assistant, and Codex is its AI coding agent that can take software engineering actions. This is similar to Microsoft&\#x27;s Windows Recall on Windows 11, which periodically captures screenshots and has faced user backlash over privacy. Unlike Recall, Computer History records only events rather than visual data.
