## [FCC Approves SpaceX to Deploy Up to 1 Million Starmind Data-Center Satellites](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/fcc-clears-spacex-million-satellites-starmind-data-center/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The U.S. Federal Communications Commission has granted SpaceX approval to launch up to one million satellites into orbits between 500 and 1,002 km to build its Starmind in-orbit data-processing network — the first time the agency has ever authorized a space data-center constellation. The authorization requires the first satellite to be in operation no later than 2033 and the entire constellation to be fully operational by 2040, and it rejected objections filed by Amazon, Viasat and the American Astronomical Society. The decision turns orbital AI compute from a pitch deck into a regulatorily sanctioned possibility, potentially moving a slice of data-center workload off the ground where power, land and local opposition constrain growth. It also puts a million-satellite scale on the table for debates over orbital traffic, debris and ground-based astronomy, and gives SpaceX a de facto first-mover position among U.S. space data-center applicants. The license carries conditions including communications backup requirements, periodic progress reports and a detailed orbital-debris mitigation plan, and SpaceX has said Starship can carry 30 to 50 Starmind satellites per launch, with a prototype targeted for early 2027 and mass production by the end of that year. The 500–1,002 km altitude band spans some of the most heavily used and most closely scrutinized low-Earth-orbit regimes, and approval is a radio-frequency and deployment license rather than a guarantee that a million satellites will actually be built.

**Background**: SpaceX confirmed Starmind in June 2026 as a project to fly AI satellites equipped with processors and solar arrays that compute in orbit instead of merely relaying data, with Elon Musk arguing that space offers effectively unlimited solar energy and no land-owning neighbours to file objections. The broader idea of &quot;space data centers&quot; is to place compute payloads in orbit so that data — for example imagery from Earth-observation satellites — can be processed on orbit rather than downlinked to ground stations. The FCC licenses radio operations and satellite deployments for U.S. operators, and applications for space data-center satellites filed with the agency now total more than 1.2 million.

## [Huawei to Detail Next-Gen HBM Chip With Double the Data Pin Density](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/huawei-teases-next-gen-ai-memory-chip-to-take-on-samsung-hynix) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Huawei plans to present a research paper at a conference in San Francisco in February detailing a next-generation HBM \(high bandwidth memory\) chip whose data pin density is claimed to be twice that of the production parts sold by Samsung and SK Hynix, according to Bloomberg. The paper&\#x27;s title alone makes the claim, and no shipping product or mass-production timeline has been announced. HBM is the memory bottleneck for AI accelerators, and the market is currently dominated by Samsung and SK Hynix, so a credible Chinese alternative with higher pin density would matter a great deal for Huawei&\#x27;s domestic AI chip ambitions. Because HBM is restricted under US export controls, progress by Huawei also signals how far China&\#x27;s push for a self-sufficient memory supply chain has advanced. The disclosure is a research-paper teaser rather than a product launch: the claim rests on the paper&\#x27;s title, with no data on capacity per stack, bandwidth figures, yields, or manufacturing partners. Pin density is only one factor in real-world HBM performance, since power, packaging, thermals and stacking yield also determine whether a design can be produced at volume.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 26, 05:30

**Background**: HBM is a type of high-performance DRAM built by stacking multiple DRAM dies vertically and connecting them with through-silicon vias \(TSV\), giving it a very wide interface and far higher bandwidth than conventional GDDR memory — which is why it sits next to AI GPUs. Data pin density refers to how many data pins an interface provides per unit of stack or die area; more pins generally mean wider data paths and higher theoretical bandwidth. Samsung and SK Hynix are the main volume suppliers of HBM, and US export controls have limited Chinese firms&\#x27; access to advanced memory and the tools needed to make it, prompting domestic efforts such as hybrid-bonding-based HBM designs.

## [Neuralink Wins FDA Clearance for First US Pivotal BCI Trial in ALS](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/neuralink-gets-clearance-for-brain-implant-trial-in-als-patients?srnd=phx-technology) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Neuralink announced that the FDA has cleared it to launch &quot;LINK,&quot; the first US pivotal clinical trial of an implantable brain-computer interface, recruiting patients with severe paralysis caused by ALS \(amyotrophic lateral sclerosis\). The trial aims to turn brain signals directly into computer control commands, and its data are intended to serve as the core evidence for a future commercial medical-device approval. This is the first FDA-cleared pivotal BCI trial in the United States, marking Neuralink&\#x27;s shift from early safety validation to commercialization-oriented data collection. A successful result would establish a regulatory pathway for implantable BCIs as approved assistive technology, with direct implications for severely paralyzed ALS patients and for competitors in the neurotechnology sector. Neuralink has previously implanted its device in more than 30 paralyzed patients worldwide through earlier studies, and trial participants undergo surgery under general anesthesia followed by device training and follow-up assessments. The company has not disclosed the number of LINK participants or the full eligibility criteria beyond severe paralysis from ALS, and details of the approved clinical protocol and primary endpoints have not been made public.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 26, 07:15

**Background**: A brain-computer interface \(BCI\) is a system that records neural activity and decodes it into commands, allowing a person to control a computer or other device without moving — typically through electrodes implanted in the brain and a wireless link to an external unit. In a pivotal trial, the sponsor gathers the primary efficacy and safety evidence that regulators use to decide whether to approve a device for the market, usually after smaller feasibility studies. ALS is a progressive neurodegenerative disease that destroys the motor neurons controlling voluntary muscles, leaving many patients cognitively intact but unable to move or speak, which makes them a key target population for assistive BCIs.

Nvidia has asked major manufacturing partners — including Dell and Supermicro, which assemble Nvidia chips into complete server systems — to pause AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, and buyers in those four countries have been told their orders cannot leave the factory. Three people familiar with the matter said the freeze is tied to a new round of US export controls, though it remains unclear whether the measure is temporary. The four countries have served as transshipment hubs through which Chinese AI companies obtain Nvidia&\#x27;s most advanced processors, which cannot be sold directly to mainland China, so the pause shows Washington&\#x27;s controls now reach third-country logistics rather than just direct sales. It could reshape AI hardware supply chains across Southeast Asia and affect regional data-center buildouts and cloud providers that depend on Nvidia-based servers. The US Commerce Department issued a new rule last week requiring regional cloud service providers to obtain export licenses within 90 days and to verify that their customers do not include restricted Chinese entities. The report does not specify which server configurations are affected or how long the hold will last, leaving open whether only top-end systems or all Nvidia-based AI servers are covered.

**Background**: Since 2022 the United States has progressively tightened export controls on advanced computing chips and semiconductor equipment destined for China, citing national security concerns; those rules bar direct sales of the most capable AI processors to mainland Chinese buyers. Because direct sales are blocked, some demand is routed through third countries via transshipment — shipping goods to an intermediate destination and then onward to the final user, which is normally a legal and routine part of world trade but can also disguise the true end customer. Washington has been widening the scope of its rules beyond physical hardware, and the latest measure extends obligations to regional cloud providers that offer access to restricted computing power. Southeast Asian countries have simultaneously become fast-growing data-center markets, making them both legitimate customers and potential diversion points.

## [Dutch Minister Rejects US Bill Restricting ASML China Sales](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/netherlands-plans-to-fight-rollout-of-proposed-us-curbs-on-asml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On November 26, Dutch Minister for Foreign Trade and Development Cooperation Sjoerd Sjoerdsma told parliament that the Netherlands will not accept further US restrictions on ASML&\#x27;s sales to China, rejecting the extraterritorial reach of the proposed MATCH Act and saying the Netherlands &quot;will not be pushed around.&quot; The Dutch government is coordinating with the European Commission and lobbying alongside Germany and France to strip the measure from the US annual defense policy bill. This is the first time a Dutch cabinet minister has so openly and publicly refused to follow Washington&\#x27;s lead on chip equipment export controls, potentially opening a rare rift among allied governments on China tech policy. The outcome directly affects ASML, whose tools underpin advanced chipmaking worldwide, and could reshape how far the US can push unilateral controls onto allied companies. The MATCH Act, introduced with bipartisan backing, would require allies such as the Netherlands and Japan to impose stricter export controls on China or face unilateral US restrictions on their semiconductor equipment; China accounts for roughly 20% of ASML&\#x27;s sales this year. Congress is expected to vote in mid-December, and the bill is widely seen as likely to pass.

**Background**: ASML is the Dutch company that is the world&\#x27;s sole supplier of extreme ultraviolet \(EUV\) lithography machines, which print chip features using 13.5 nm light and are essential for producing the most advanced semiconductors. The US has long used export controls — including the extraterritorial Foreign Direct Product Rule — to limit Chinese access to such equipment, and has pressed allies like the Netherlands and Japan to align their rules. The MATCH Act would formalize that pressure by tying allied compliance to continued access to the US market and US technology.

## [Cloudflare Workers Free Plan Now Allows Email Sending to Verified Addresses](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-11-24-email-sending-workers-free/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Cloudflare announced that Email Sending is now available on the Workers Free plan, letting developers send email to verified destination addresses in their account through a Workers binding, the REST API, or SMTP. Messages sent to verified addresses are free on every plan and do not count against monthly quotas or daily send limits, while sending to arbitrary recipients still requires the Workers Paid plan. Transactional email has historically been one of the few things a Worker could not do without either a paid tier or a third-party email provider, so this removes a common friction point for prototyping, internal alerting, and development workflows. It also makes Cloudflare&\#x27;s email stack more self-contained for developers already hosting domains and Workers on the platform. The free allowance is scoped to verified recipient addresses registered in the account rather than to a fixed number of messages, so it is well suited to developer and staging use but not to production traffic to arbitrary end users. Unverified recipients silently become the boundary of the paid plan, so teams will need to upgrade before shipping customer-facing mail.

**Background**: Cloudflare Workers is a serverless platform that runs JavaScript and other languages at the edge, and &quot;bindings&quot; are how a Worker securely reaches Cloudflare resources such as KV, R2, D1, or email without embedding API keys or secrets in code. Before this change, developers who wanted to send mail from a Worker typically called a third-party transactional email API, such as MailChannels, which required the sending domain to be on Cloudflare and a DNS TXT record for verification. Cloudflare already offered inbound email handling through Email Routing, so outbound sending rounds out the picture on the same platform.

## [ASML CEO warns full China cutoff would accelerate China&\#x27;s own lithography](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/25/2026/asml-makes-the-case-for-selling-chips-to-china) ⭐️ 7.0/10

ASML CEO Christophe Fouquet said on Semafor&\#x27;s Future Proof that export controls should protect the most advanced chipmaking technology but should not cut China off entirely, warning that a full ban would push China to double or triple its investment in self-developed lithography. He argued that ASML&\#x27;s China sales, which lag the leading edge by more than a decade, preserve that technology gap and keep China dependent on Western equipment. The remarks frame a central policy choice: broad export bans may slow China in the short term but could accelerate a costly indigenous toolchain, while a deliberately maintained technology gap aims to preserve leverage. The outcome will affect ASML&\#x27;s revenue, Dutch and U.S. export-control coordination, and the pace of China&\#x27;s semiconductor self-sufficiency. ASML has held close to 100% of China&\#x27;s lithography market for nearly 30 years, according to Fouquet, and the company and the Dutch government oppose the MATCH Act, which would extend U.S.-aligned controls from EUV to immersion DUV and maintenance services.

**Background**: Lithography is the step that prints circuit patterns onto silicon wafers. ASML is the only supplier of EUV lithography systems, and the MATCH Act is a U.S. bipartisan bill meant to close gaps between U.S. export controls and those of allied countries.
