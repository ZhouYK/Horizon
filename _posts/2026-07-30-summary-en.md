On July 30, 2026, Google DeepMind unveiled Gemini Robotics 2, a suite of models that for the first time enables whole-body intelligent control of full humanoid robots, including walking, squatting, and grasping. The suite includes a Vision-Language-Action \(VLA\) model, an Embodied Reasoning model \(ER 2\), and an on-device model, and also supports multi-robot collaboration. This represents a major leap in embodied AI, moving beyond simple manipulation to whole-body coordination and long-horizon task planning. By opening ER 2 via Gemini API and Google AI Studio, DeepMind is democratizing advanced robotics capabilities for developers and researchers. ER 2 acts as a &\#x27;high-level brain&\#x27; for multi-step planning involving hundreds of decisions over minutes, while the on-device model can adapt to new robot bodies with fewer than 200 examples and just hours of training. Additionally, the ASIMOV-Agentic safety benchmark was introduced to enable automatic safe stopping when humans approach.

These advances build on Google DeepMind&\#x27;s earlier work like RT-2, which pioneered end-to-end robot control from VLMs.
