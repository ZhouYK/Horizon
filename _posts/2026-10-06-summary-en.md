## [2026 Nobel Prize in Physics Goes to Francis Halzen for IceCube](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2026/press-release/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On October 6, 2026, the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences announced that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics was awarded to Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison for his decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. Halzen first proposed detecting neutrinos in Antarctic ice in 1988, led the construction of the roughly one-cubic-kilometer detector, and saw it begin capturing cosmic high-energy neutrinos after completion, with the prize carrying 12 million Swedish kronor. This recognizes a paradigm-shifting discovery that founded neutrino astronomy: because neutrinos travel essentially unimpeded through matter and radiation, they can reveal the highest-energy astrophysical processes that electromagnetic telescopes cannot see. The award highlights how a decades-long, multinational Antarctic instrument turned a speculative idea into a new observational window on the universe, affecting astrophysics, particle physics, and future multi-messenger astronomy. IceCube embeds thousands of spherical digital optical modules on strings in the Antarctic ice at depths between about 1,450 and 2,450 meters, looking for TeV-range neutrino interactions that produce faint Cherenkov light. Detecting astrophysical neutrinos requires extremely high energies \(above roughly 100 TeV\) because their expected fluxes are so low, and IceCube&\#x27;s first major expansion, an upgrade approved in 2019, was announced as successfully deployed in February 2026.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 6, 09:54

**Background**: Neutrinos are nearly massless subatomic particles that interact only via the weak nuclear force, so they pass through planets and stars almost untouched — earning them the nickname &quot;ghost particles.&quot; This makes them extremely hard to detect, but also ideal messengers carrying information from violent cosmic sources such as active galactic nuclei and gamma-ray bursts. IceCube, developed by the University of Wisconsin–Madison and built at the Amundsen–Scott South Pole Station, uses the vast transparent Antarctic ice as its detection medium, watching for the flashes of light created on the rare occasions a neutrino interacts with an ice nucleus.

## [Pure Gasoline Cars Drop Below 50% of Global New Car Sales](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/automobiles/gas-vehicles-fall-under-50-of-global-new-auto-sales-for-first-time) ⭐️ 7.0/10

In the first half of 2026, global sales of pure gasoline-powered vehicles \(excluding hybrids and other electrified models\) fell 10% year-on-year to 20.25 million units, accounting for 49% of global new car sales — down 3 percentage points and below the 50% mark for the first time. Over the same period, global battery electric vehicle \(BEV\) sales rose 12% to 6.87 million units, lifting their share to 17%, according to Nikkei Asia. This is a structural milestone for the auto and energy transition: the internal combustion engine has lost its status as the default choice for more than half of the world&\#x27;s new car buyers. It matters for automakers planning production and powertrain investment, for oil demand forecasts, and for the battery and charging supply chains that scale with BEV volumes. The shift was partly driven by higher oil prices linked to the Middle East conflict, which dampened demand for fuel-burning cars, while regional BEV trends diverged sharply — electric sales declined in China and North America but grew in Europe. Even so, pure gasoline cars still account for just under half of all new vehicles sold, with hybrids, plug-in hybrids and other electrified models making up much of the remainder.

## [Honda and Taisei Develop In-Motion Wireless EV Charging Technology](https://china.kyodonews.net/articles/-/16535) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Honda and Taisei Corporation announced they have jointly developed a basic technology for wirelessly supplying power to pure electric vehicles while they are driving, with a vehicle passing over ground-embedded power units at roughly 80 km/h expected to momentarily receive up to 150 kW. The two companies plan to conduct proof-of-concept testing on the Tateyama Expressway in Chiba Prefecture from fiscal 2027 onward. If it works at highway speed, in-motion charging could shrink EV battery packs, cut charging downtime for logistics fleets, and turn highways themselves into energy infrastructure — a strategic shift for Japan, which is pushing hard to keep its automotive industry competitive in electrification. Success would benefit trucking and long-haul transport first, since those operators are most penalized by today&\#x27;s charging stops and range limits. The figures cited — up to 150 kW at about 80 km/h — describe instantaneous power transfer from the road to the vehicle, and the effort is still at the &quot;basic technology&quot; stage, with no peer-reviewed results or efficiency, alignment-tolerance, or cost data disclosed. The demonstration is tied to a specific expressway section from fiscal 2027, and Honda says it aims for practical deployment in logistics and transport while Taisei stresses the importance of working with automakers and integrating the technology into road systems.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 6, 08:18

**Background**: Wireless charging for EVs comes in two main forms: static charging, where a parked car sits over a charging pad, and dynamic \(in-motion\) charging, where power transfer coils are embedded in the roadway so a moving vehicle picks up energy as it drives. Dynamic wireless power transfer, also called in-motion wireless power transfer \(IM-WPT\), has been studied for years and tested on pilot roadways, including a Purdue University testbed backed by the Indiana Department of Transportation, but it remains challenging because efficient transfer requires precise coil alignment at speed and costly road reconstruction.

## [sub2api Payment Flaw: Forged EasyPay Callbacks Allow Free Recharge](https://github.com/Wei-Shaw/sub2api/issues/7881) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A security issue \(GitHub issue \#7881\) reported on the open-source sub2api project claims that attackers can replay the order-creation signature to forge a successful EasyPay callback, causing unpaid recharge orders to be marked as paid without possessing the merchant key; any ordinary registered account with recharge privileges could trigger it. The author recommends switching away from popup mode as a mitigation until the flaw is fixed. If confirmed, the flaw is a payment-integrity defect that lets users obtain paid balance for free, directly threatening the revenue and accounting of self-hosted sub2api deployments that enable EasyPay. Open-source projects geared toward individual operators rarely receive security review, so a disclosed signature-bypass attack chain gives affected operators an actionable reason to audit and patch their payment flow. The attack chain reportedly stems from multiple weaknesses combining: signature strings are concatenated without escaping, query parameters carried in return\_url are not sanitized, and popup mode \(submit.php\) exposes the order signature directly in the payment URL, with callback parameter validation being too lax. The issue only affects deployments that both enable EasyPay and integrate it in popup mode, and it remains open and unverified pending deeper technical analysis.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 6, 13:31

**Background**: sub2api is a self-hosted open-source project that provides an API gateway/relay and account recharge functionality for individual operators. EasyPay is a widely used aggregate payment interface favored by individual developers because it supports no-contract payment collection; it verifies the authenticity of asynchronous payment notifications by comparing a merchant-key-based signature. In the popup integration mode the payment URL includes the order signature, so if the same signature can be reused to forge a success callback, the server may credit a recharge that was never actually paid.
