## [OpenAI&\#x27;s Astra Makes Progress on Ten Long-Standing Math Problems](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

OpenAI announced that an internal version of its Astra model produced new results on ten long-open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science, including high-dimensional sphere packing, existence of non-sofic groups, and a counterexample to Connes&\#x27; rigidity conjecture. The arguments were human-assisted and formalized in the Lean proof assistant. The US sanctions list currently includes Iran, Cuba, North Korea, and occupied territories of Ukraine.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 1, 03:08

**Background**: Android developer verification is a new security layer Google is rolling out that links real-world identities to Android apps; starting in 2026, apps installed on certified Android devices must be registered by a verified developer, including apps distributed outside the Play Store. The system is designed to deter bad actors who use sideloading or third-party stores to distribute harmful apps. The sanction exemption is an exception to this rule for devices in sanctioned regions, so developers there can keep distributing apps without going through the verification process.
