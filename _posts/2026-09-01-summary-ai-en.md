## [DeepSeek Unveils First Open-Source Multimodal Model for Agent Tasks](https://www.aibase.com/news/30755) ⭐️ 9.0/10

DeepSeek released its first experimental multimodal model, DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, on Hugging Face under the MIT license, featuring 305B parameters. Built on the V4-Flash-0731 backbone, it integrates a vision encoder and an Aligner to gain image understanding through continued training. This marks a significant expansion of DeepSeek&\#x27;s open-source ecosystem into multimodal AI, shifting focus from traditional image captioning and VQA toward agent-centric vision. A permissively licensed 305B model designed for multimodal agent tasks could accelerate research and real-world deployment in UI navigation, document understanding, and tool use. Unlike conventional vision-language models that emphasize VQA, this experimental model is specifically tailored for multimodal agent capabilities. According to its vLLM recipe, it combines the V4-Flash MoE backbone with a 32-layer vision tower, supports a 1M context length, and the 285B/13B MoE variant scores 83.5% on OCRBench.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 1, 16:01

**Background**: Multimodal agents are AI systems that can process and respond to multiple data types simultaneously, such as text, images, audio, and video. A vision-language model typically combines a vision encoder, like a Vision Transformer, with a large language model, and an Aligner or projection layer bridges the gap between visual and textual representations. DeepSeek&\#x27;s V4 series is its latest open-source LLM family, and this multimodal variant extends it with agent-oriented visual understanding. Such agent-centric design prioritizes reading screens, following UI layouts, and executing tasks rather than simply describing images.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 1, 15:01

Collective jailbreak attacks, where many agents are manipulated simultaneously, are a growing concern in the AI security community.
