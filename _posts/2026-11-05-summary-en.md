The decision derails SoftBank&\#x27;s plan to build out a position spanning artificial intelligence and industrial robotics. China accounts for roughly 30% of ABB&\#x27;s robotics business, so a blocking decision by Beijing can effectively determine whether a global industrial-robotics deal is viable at all, not merely how it is structured locally. ## [Hydrogen Chloride Detected Above Martian Fissures Hints at Ongoing Volcanism](https://wsau.com/2026/11/04/pungent-gas-above-martian-fissures-hints-at-ongoing-volcanism/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The ESA-Roscosmos ExoMars Trace Gas Orbiter \(TGO\) has detected hydrogen chloride \(HCl\) in the lower atmosphere above surface fissures in the Alba Mons region of Mars, and the research team concludes that volcanic outgassing is the most likely source. The finding, published in Science Advances, shows the gas peaking each year in early summer, which the team attributes to ice inside the fissures melting and allowing magmatic gases from below to escape. If a subsurface magma source is indeed still present, the associated heat could sustain hydrothermal environments potentially habitable for microbes, which would mean Mars&\#x27;s volcanism — and its habitability window — extended far more recently than commonly assumed. The result also gives planetary scientists a new, seasonally recurring geochemical signal to hunt for active geology on a planet long thought to be volcanically dead. The detection was made in the lowermost atmosphere right above the fissures, and the gas appears as a seasonal peak in early Martian summer rather than a constant background, pointing to a repeating release mechanism tied to warming and ice melt. HCl is a short-lived tracer, so its recurring presence requires a continuing source rather than a relic from an ancient eruption.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 5, 12:42

**Background**: The ExoMars Trace Gas Orbiter is a European Space Agency–Roscosmos mission launched in 2016, carrying spectrometers such as NOMAD and ACS that identify trace gases in the Martian atmosphere at very low concentrations. Alba Mons, in the northern Tharsis region, is the largest volcano on Mars by surface area, with volcanic flow fields stretching at least 1,350 km from its summit. Trace gases are closely watched on Mars because they can be produced by geological activity such as outgassing, or by living organisms, and because they disappear quickly unless something keeps replenishing them.

## [OpenSSH rushes 10.6p2 fix for Alpine long-password auth bypass](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/openssh-issues-emergency-fix-for-flaw.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On November 5, OpenSSH released version 10.6p2 to fix a vulnerability that let an attacker bypass SSH password authentication by submitting an oversized password of roughly 257 to 1024 bytes. On affected systems — Alpine Linux and other musl-based distributions where sshd is built and run with PAM support disabled — this allows logging into accounts that have no password set at all, though the attacker still needs network access to the server and a valid account name. Alpine has shipped patched packages for versions 3.16 and later, and no exploitation has been reported so far. An authentication bypass in OpenSSH, the near-universal remote login daemon, is high-value security news: Alpine is the default base image for a huge share of container images, so many key-only servers and CI environments are exposed. The fact that a single password-enabled account on a host can make every password-less account reachable turns a narrow bug into a broad exposure for anyone running Alpine containers or servers with sshd. The root cause is considered to lie outside sshd — a musl libc crypt\(\) truncation issue combined with OpenSSH&\#x27;s password handling — which is why glibc-based systems fail closed and are not exploitable the same way. Users who cannot upgrade immediately can set &quot;PasswordAuthentication no&quot; in sshd\_config and restart sshd as a workaround; the official OpenSSH release notes for 10.6p2 describe the bug as only exploitable where PAM support is disabled.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 5, 16:44

**Background**: SSH is the standard protocol for encrypted remote login, and OpenSSH is its dominant implementation; servers commonly disable password logins entirely in favor of public-key authentication, so an account may exist with no usable password. PAM \(Pluggable Authentication Modules\) is the Linux framework sshd normally delegates password checking to, and musl libc is the lightweight C library Alpine uses instead of the more common glibc, with its own crypt\(\) implementation for hashing passwords. Alpine&\#x27;s minimal footprint makes it a favorite base image for containers and appliances such as OpenWrt routers, which is why a libc-level quirk can have wide reach.

## [OpenAI releases GPT-6 Cyber, a security-focused model powering Linux kernel research](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2118390190246879554) ⭐️ 7.0/10

OpenAI has released GPT-6 Cyber, a cyber-specialized variant of GPT-6 Astra trained for authorized security work such as vulnerability discovery, exploit development, and web pentesting. The model powered OpenAI&\#x27;s Linux kernel security study, in which autonomous agents reportedly identified 948 kernel flaws and built working root exploits, and OpenAI says security teams at Mozilla, Palo Alto Networks, and Cisco are already using it. Access is currently limited to select Daybreak Red customers, with OpenAI promising to expand availability gradually. This marks OpenAI&\#x27;s most aggressive push yet into offensive-adjacent security tooling, packaging frontier vulnerability-research capability behind a vetted-access program rather than open API availability. If the reported results hold, automated discovery-to-patch pipelines could dramatically compress the time between finding and fixing flaws in foundational software like the Linux kernel, which underpins Android and billions of devices. OpenAI&\#x27;s study reported 948 unique Linux kernel flaws and 10 previously undisclosed exploit techniques found in a single scan, with agents also generating patches at roughly $1,280 per vulnerability and limited human intervention. GPT-6 Cyber uses specialized post-training to boost cybersecurity capability and reduce cyber refusals, but is scoped to stay within the authorization a researcher specifies, as detailed in its published system card.

**Background**: Daybreak is OpenAI&\#x27;s &quot;Trusted Access for Cyber&quot; program, which since August 10, 2026 has been split into two tiers: Daybreak Blue, which gives vetted defenders a de-guardrailed version of GPT-5.6 Sol, and Daybreak Red, which offers purpose-trained cyber models like GPT-5.5-Cyber with reduced refusals. GPT-6 Cyber is the successor in that Red lineage, built on the GPT-6 Astra base model. Vendors increasingly ship cyber-specialized models because general-purpose refusal behavior blocks legitimate vulnerability research, while unrestricted offensive capability raises misuse risks that tiered vetting is meant to contain.

## [Xiaomi trials robot agent system in staff apartment building](https://weibo.com/1749127163/5361796274981767) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Xiaomi has started trial operation of a robot agent system built on its Xiaomi-Robotics-1 embodied foundation model in its Xiaomi Youth Apartment building. The system closes a loop spanning data collection, model inference and task execution, autonomously planning and completing desk tidying, trash sweeping, item sorting and return, room inspection and whole-home re-inspection on runs lasting over 30 minutes, across 3 rooms and 50+ tasks. Most embodied AI demos are short, single-task tabletop manipulations in lab settings, so a 30-plus-minute, multi-room autonomous run in a real apartment is a meaningful step toward deployable long-horizon household robotics. If it holds up, this kind of agent loop is what turns a foundation model into an actual product for home and commercial service robots. The disclosed metrics are limited to scale rather than quality: 30+ minutes of continuous operation, 3 rooms and 50+ tasks, with no success rate, intervention count, or benchmark comparison published. The post is a brief promotional summary attributed to Lei Jun and gives no information on hardware platform, safety fallbacks, or how often a human had to step in.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 5, 08:19

**Background**: Xiaomi-Robotics-1 is Xiaomi&\#x27;s Vision-Language-Action \(VLA\) foundation model for robots, pre-trained on roughly 100,000 hours of real-world manipulation trajectories spanning more than 1,700 scenarios such as households, commercial premises, industrial sites and outdoor spaces, and designed to work out of the box in unseen environments. A &\#x27;long-horizon&\#x27; task means chaining many sub-goals in sequence — clearing a table, for instance, requires picking up plates, dumping scraps and loading the sink in order — so error accumulation over time is the core difficulty. Agent systems wrap such a model in a planning-and-execution loop so it can decide what to do next rather than only reacting step by step.

## [Micron Pauses $4.5B Taoyuan Plant Amid Taiwan Labor Dispute](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-05/micron-pauses-work-on-4-5-billion-taiwan-plant-after-labor-spat) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Micron has paused construction of its $4.5 billion memory chip plant in Taoyuan, Taiwan, and has notified Taiwanese officials and suppliers of the halt. Executives are concerned that agreements reached with local unions could be overturned; the fab had originally been slated to start production in 2028. Taiwan accounts for roughly 60% of Micron&\#x27;s DRAM output, so a prolonged construction pause could ripple through the company&\#x27;s memory capacity plans at a time when AI data centers are keeping DRAM and HBM demand tight. It also highlights labor relations as a growing risk factor in semiconductor expansion, not just land, power and water. The Taoyuan union is demanding that 15% of operating profit be distributed to employees worldwide and is reportedly trying to overturn an agreement previously reached with the Taichung union. Micron says Taiwan remains important to its global footprint and that existing operations are running normally, so this is a pause rather than a cancellation.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 5, 11:26

**Background**: DRAM is the mainstream volatile memory chip used as main memory in PCs, servers and phones, and HBM — the high-bandwidth memory that AI accelerators depend on — is built by vertically stacking multiple DRAM dies. Micron runs major DRAM fabs in Taiwan, including Taoyuan and Taichung, and has used its Taiwan fabs to mass-produce advanced nodes such as 1αnm DRAM. New fabs typically take several years from groundbreaking to volume production, which is why a pause on a plant targeted for 2028 output matters even before any silicon is made.
