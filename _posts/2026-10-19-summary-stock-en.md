## [PBOC Creates Non-Bank Redemption Liquidity Backstop, Expands SLF](https://finance.caixin.com/2026-10-19/102494878.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

At the opening of the 2026 Financial Street Forum on October 19, PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng announced that the central bank, together with the National Financial Regulatory Administration and the CSRC, will establish a liquidity support tool for non-bank institutions facing large-scale redemptions, covering bank wealth management subsidiaries, public fund managers, and their parent banks and securities firms. The same package includes expanding the range of institutions eligible for standing lending facility \(SLF\) operations, implementing interbank-exchange bond market connectivity, and further facilitating RMB purchase and sale business by foreign financial institutions in the onshore FX market. This is the first time the PBOC has built a dedicated backstop for asset managers in mass-redemption scenarios, explicitly designed to break the &quot;redemption-sale&quot; negative feedback loop in the bond market and framed as a new macro-prudential instrument. It directly affects how bond funds and wealth management products are priced and managed for liquidity risk, and it gives the central bank a standing lever over system-wide bond market stress rather than only ad hoc crisis response. The tool is to be activated only in specific scenarios where asset managers face group-wide liquidity risk and the bond market enters a redemption-sale spiral, and institutions using it must meet macro-prudential requirements and pledge high-grade collateral so that market pricing is not distorted and no &quot;bailout illusion&quot; is created. Alongside it, the SLF — the facility that anchors the upper bound of the PBOC&\#x27;s interest rate corridor — will be extended to a wider set of eligible institutions.

**Background**: The SLF is a central bank lending facility through which financial institutions borrow liquidity against qualified collateral, and its interest rate serves as the ceiling of the PBOC&\#x27;s rate corridor. The PBOC already built a precedent for non-bank liquidity backstops in October 2024, when it created the securities, fund and insurance companies swap facility \(SFISF\) allowing non-bank institutions to pledge bonds, stock ETFs and CSI 300 constituents in exchange for emergency liquidity. Separately, connecting the interbank and exchange-traded bond markets has been a long-standing reform goal, with joint PBOC-CSRC rules for cross-market trading published in 2022 to create a more unified market and price formation.
