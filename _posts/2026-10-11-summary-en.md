## [AI Vibe-Coding Decompilation Spawns Flawless Browser Ports of PS2/PS3 Games](https://kotaku.com/we-might-be-cooked-as-these-vibe-coded-web-browser-ports-of-halo-the-simpsons-hit-and-run-and-gta-vice-city-seem-to-work-perfectly-2000743300) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Over recent weeks, hundreds of AI vibe-coded decompilation projects have appeared at once, producing browser-based emulators and ports of PS2/PS3-era titles such as Call of Duty: Black Ops, Halo: Combat Evolved and GTA: Vice City that run close to perfectly — with at least one Halo port even offering online multiplayer servers. Kotaku reports that Claude Opus 5.5 is so strong at decompilation that source-code reconstruction which previously took months of human effort can now be finished in a single overnight AI run. The shift could fundamentally change the economics of reverse engineering and game preservation: tasks that once required a multi-month community effort are collapsing into overnight automated runs, which also means publishers&\#x27; takedown letters and DMCA notices are becoming a game of whack-a-mole. It affects ROM hackers, emulator developers, preservationists and rights holders alike, and follows the broader trend of AI-assisted development spreading from ordinary software into legally grey territory. Memory is no longer the limiting factor — the PS3 had roughly 512 MB of RAM, while the browser alone now consumes about 1200 MB — so the real bottleneck was always the months of human decompilation labour that AI now absorbs. One caveat worth noting is that matching a high percentage of functions does not equal producing fully readable, maintainable source code, a gap visible in AI-assisted projects such as the Super Mario 64 DS reconstruction, which reported 99.9% of functions matched but only about 30.5% converted into readable form.

**Background**: Decompilation is the process of converting a compiled binary back into human-readable source code, traditionally done by communities of hobbyist reverse engineers who spend months or years on a single game; emulators, by contrast, run the original binary rather than recreating its source. Vibe coding, a term coined by Andrej Karpathy in February 2025, describes development where a developer describes what they want in natural language and an LLM writes the code, often without close review. Claude Opus 5.5 is the flagship model of Anthropic&\#x27;s Claude 5.5 generation, and AI-assisted decompilation tools such as Ghidra-inspired AI decompilers combine static binary analysis \(LIEF, Capstone\) with LLM code generation to produce C/C++ from executables.

## [Japan Declares &\#x27;Cyberspace State of Emergency&\#x27; After Attacks on 20+ Firms](https://www.ft.com/content/2d4333d1-8119-44a4-a5a5-2ee9ef2544fa?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Japan&\#x27;s government declared a &quot;cyberspace state of emergency&quot; after cyberattacks hit more than 20 major organizations in recent weeks, spanning rail, automotive, healthcare and government facilities, with potentially tens of millions of customer records leaked. In response, the Financial Services Agency ordered banks to stop accepting driver&\#x27;s licenses as identity proof for opening new accounts and to require chip-equipped identification instead. This marks an unusually public escalation for Japan, which has historically been seen as a low-incident cyber target, and signals that attacks are now reaching critical infrastructure and core financial onboarding processes rather than isolated corporate networks. The forced change to identity verification at banks affects everyday consumers nationwide and could become a template for tighter digital identity rules across other sectors. JR East said its ticketing system may have leaked the email addresses of 1.67 million customers, and several of the incidents are linked to a ransomware attack on IDC Frontier, a SoftBank Group cloud subsidiary whose outage affected hundreds of corporate and local government customers. Experts describe Japan&\#x27;s defenses as weak and note that AI is lowering the barrier to sophisticated attacks, though there is no evidence yet that the latest incidents involved AI.

The identity-verification change relates to Japan&\#x27;s &quot;My Number Card,&quot; a national ID with an embedded IC chip that supports banking, tax filing and healthcare services, and which is harder to forge than a paper document like a driver&\#x27;s license. Japan has also been developing an &quot;active cyber defense&quot; framework to strengthen coordination between its Self-Defense Forces and allies on state-sponsored cyber threats.

## [Study: 10 Minutes of AI Use Weakens Persistence on Hard Tasks](https://news.berkeley.edu/2026/10/09/using-ai-for-just-10-minutes-erodes-your-ability-to-persist-at-hard-things/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A study involving UC Berkeley researchers found that after just 10 minutes of using an AI tool such as ChatGPT, participants&\#x27; accuracy and persistence on difficult tasks dropped sharply once the AI was taken away. The experiment recruited 1,222 participants and tested them on fraction arithmetic problems and SAT reading comprehension questions. The findings feed a fast-growing debate about AI dependency in education and knowledge work, suggesting that even brief AI assistance can erode the mental stamina needed for hard problem-solving. If replicated, the result supports redesigning AI tools as tutors that scaffold reasoning rather than as systems that complete tasks on the user&\#x27;s behalf. Participants first solved problems with ChatGPT&\#x27;s help, and after the tool was removed some answered incorrectly or gave up entirely, indicating a drop in both correctness and willingness to keep trying. The publicly available summary does not detail the peer-review status, the control conditions, or whether the effect persists over longer periods, so the durability of the observed decline remains an open question.

**Background**: Psychologists describe handing mental work to external tools — from finger-counting to search engines — as &\#x27;cognitive offloading,&\#x27; a strategy that saves internal cognitive resources but can reduce the need to practice a skill. Recent research on generative AI in universities distinguishes &\#x27;tool dependency&\#x27; from deeper &\#x27;cognitive dependency,&\#x27; the latter being more strongly linked to cognitive inertia. At the same time, controlled studies of ChatGPT-assisted learning have found genuine learning gains when the AI provides help rather than answers, which is why researchers here recommend a tutoring-style role for AI.
