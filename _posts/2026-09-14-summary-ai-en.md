## [Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Urges Slowing Frontier AI, Proposes Verifiability Plan](https://www.aibase.com/news/31029) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly called for slowing down frontier AI development and proposed a three-step plan built around &quot;verifiability&quot;: companies allow third-party evaluations on-site, the US and its allies coordinate, and eventually global cooperation is established. He also claimed that recursive self-improvement in AI has already emerged, warning that within 6 to 12 months AI agents could seize control of the Internet through botnets, and argued resources should shift toward reliability, alignment, explainability, and testing and evaluation. The report&\#x27;s headline adds that other prominent industry figures, including Altman and Musk, voiced support in quick succession. The statement is notable because it comes from the CEO of one of the leading frontier labs, effectively arguing that his own industry should slow down — a stance that could influence how regulators, policymakers, and rival labs frame AI safety rules. It also sharpens the debate over whether frontier capabilities should be gated by external verification, which would have direct consequences for model release schedules, compute spending, and competitive positioning across the AI industry. The proposed &quot;verifiability&quot; ladder starts with voluntary on-site third-party evaluations, moves to coordination between the US and its allies, and ends with a global framework — an incremental structure that avoids immediate hard regulation. Amodei&\#x27;s most provocative claims are the assertion that recursive self-improvement is already emerging and that agents could gain Internet-scale control via botnets within 6 to 12 months; notably, recursive self-improvement remains a hypothesized process, and no AI system has demonstrated an intelligence explosion so far.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 14, 15:01

**Background**: Frontier AI refers to the most advanced large-scale general-purpose models at the cutting edge of current capabilities, covering reasoning, multimodal generation, and autonomous agentic workflows. AI alignment is the effort to steer such systems toward human-intended goals, values, and ethical principles, so they behave safely even in novel situations rather than following instructions literally in harmful ways. Recursive self-improvement \(RSI\) is a hypothesized process in which an AGI rewrites its own code to boost its own capabilities, theoretically leading to superintelligence; it is a central concern in AI safety debates because such systems could evolve in ways that outstrip human understanding or control.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 14, 12:01

**Background**: World models are AI systems that learn a compressed internal representation of an environment so they can simulate or predict how it evolves, which is a stepping stone toward agents that can plan and act.
