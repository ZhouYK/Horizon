Washington&\#x27;s attitude matters because Japan has in the past faced criticism that weak-yen policies amount to competitive devaluation, and US Treasury support makes intervention both more effective and less diplomatically costly.

## [PBOC warns of FX herd behavior, keeps moderately loose policy](https://finance.caixin.com/2026-09-24/102488616.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On September 24, 2026, the People&\#x27;s Bank of China published a statement saying its Monetary Policy Committee held its 2026 third-quarter meeting on September 19, at which it reiterated continued implementation of a moderately loose monetary policy and stronger counter-cyclical adjustment. For the first time in a quarterly Monetary Policy Committee readout, the meeting also called for guarding against &quot;herd effects&quot; and the self-reinforcement of irrational expectations in the foreign exchange market. Putting FX herd behavior and self-reinforcing expectations into a Monetary Policy Committee readout for the first time signals that the central bank is unusually alert to RMB volatility and is publicly reserving the right to smooth disorderly moves. The meeting&\#x27;s parallel pledge to push forward reform of the monetary policy operating framework also points to possible changes in how the PBOC manages liquidity, policy rates and exchange-rate management over the medium term. The meeting announced no immediate change to interest rates or to quantitative easing or tightening, only a commitment to &quot;comprehensively use and adjust monetary policy tools as appropriate.&quot; The same FX wording had already been used on September 10, 2026 by PBOC Deputy Governor Lu Lei at a State Council Information Office press conference, where he described China&\#x27;s managed floating exchange rate regime as letting the market play a decisive role in rate formation while guarding against herd effects and self-reinforcing irrational expectations.

rss · 财新网 · Sep 25, 14:50

**Background**: The PBOC&\#x27;s Monetary Policy Committee meets roughly once a quarter, and its short readouts are closely parsed for shifts in official policy tone even when no concrete tool changes are announced. A &quot;herd effect&quot; in the FX market describes traders piling into the same direction and amplifying price moves, so that the exchange rate drifts away from economic fundamentals, often driven by fear of missing out. &quot;Self-reinforcing irrational expectations&quot; is the related mechanism in which an expected depreciation prompts selling, and that selling then makes the depreciation happen, validating the original belief. China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime, under which the central bank sets a daily central parity and allows trading within a band, giving authorities tools to lean against such destabilizing dynamics.
