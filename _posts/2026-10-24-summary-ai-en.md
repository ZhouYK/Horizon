## [Italian report claims mRNA melanoma vaccine keeps 90% of patients metastasis-free at 30 months](https://www.imgpress.it/attualita/melanoma-vaccino-a-mrna-90-dei-pazienti-senza-metastasi-a-30-mesi/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

An Italian news outlet, Imgpress, reported that an mRNA-based melanoma vaccine left roughly 90% of treated patients free of distant metastases at 30 months. The article presents this as a therapeutic-vaccine result but does not link to the underlying publication or name the specific candidate, which corresponds to the personalised neoantigen therapy mRNA-4157/V940 \(intismeran autogene\) given with pembrolizumab. If the figure holds up, it reinforces the case for personalised mRNA cancer vaccines as an adjuvant strategy that cuts distant recurrence in high-risk resected melanoma, a population where relapse risk has historically been high. The result also feeds into a broader wave of interest in mRNA oncology platforms beyond infectious disease, with Moderna/Merck and BioNTech both pushing candidates into late-stage trials. The underlying data come from the randomised Phase 2b KEYNOTE-942/mRNA-4157-P201 trial, which reported a significant improvement in distant metastasis-free survival with a 62% reduction in the risk of distant recurrence or death \(HR 0.384; 95% CI 0.172–0.858; P = .015\) versus pembrolizumab alone. Caveats include the modest Phase 2b sample size, the fact that the Italian article is a secondary report without peer-reviewed citation, and the distinction between distant metastasis-free survival and overall survival, which was not clearly separated in the coverage.

gdelt · imgpress.it · Oct 24, 19:15

**Background**: Personalised neoantigen mRNA vaccines work by sequencing a patient&\#x27;s tumour, identifying mutations unique to the cancer, and encoding those neoantigens in an mRNA payload that teaches the immune system to hunt down residual cancer cells after surgery. mRNA-4157/V940 \(intismeran autogene\) is developed by Moderna and Merck and is given together with the checkpoint inhibitor pembrolizumab \(Keytruda\). In melanoma, the KEYNOTE-942 trial tested this combination in patients with completely resected high-risk stage IIIB/C/D and IV disease, and a subsequent Phase 3 trial, INTerpath-001, has since reported meeting its endpoints.
