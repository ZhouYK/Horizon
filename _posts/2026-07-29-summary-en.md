## [China Bans Blue Autonomous Driving Indicator Lights from 2026](https://www.yicai.com/brief/103296987.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China will prohibit the installation of &\#x27;small blue lights&\#x27; used as external indicators for autonomous driving on newly certified vehicles starting July 27, 2026, because they violate the GB4785 standard on automotive lighting. This regulation impacts all automakers selling vehicles in China, requiring redesign of ADAS indicator systems. It clarifies the legal status of such lights and may influence similar regulations globally. The ban applies only to new vehicle type certifications from July 27, 2026; vehicles already certified before that date are unaffected. The &\#x27;small blue lights&\#x27; were first popularized by Li Auto in 2022 and later adopted by other Chinese EV makers.

Autonomous driving indicator lights are not defined in this standard, leading to their prohibition. The trend of using blue lights to indicate autonomous driving mode started in 2022 with the Li Auto L9.

## [Meta&\#x27;s $14B data center deal uses future lease as collateral, raising borrowing costs](https://www.reddit.com/r/investing/comments/1v9tyh6/metas_borrowing_costs_just_went_up_04_points_from/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Meta&\#x27;s $14 billion data center project with BlackRock is financed using bonds backed by a future 20-year lease agreement that begins in 2028, rather than the physical building. The bonds yield over 7%, about 0.4 percentage points higher than Meta&\#x27;s own bond issuance in October 2024, despite the same A+ credit rating. This structure highlights increasing caution among bond investors about AI infrastructure debt, as demand for this offering was only 1.6x oversubscribed versus the typical 4x average. It also signals higher financing costs for Big Tech&\#x27;s massive data center buildout, which may ultimately affect AI investment returns. The special-purpose vehicle Sopaipilla Holdings owns the project, with BlackRock entities holding 80% and Meta 20%. Meta contributed $2.3 billion in land and existing construction, while BlackRock provided $4.9 billion cash and arranged $12.5 billion in bonds due 2048. The off-balance-sheet structure keeps the rent obligation off Meta&\#x27;s books but does not reduce the actual liability.

reddit · r/investing · /u/iOCharts\_ · Jul 29, 11:51

**Background**: Off-balance-sheet financing, such as synthetic leases, allows companies to use assets without reporting the associated debt on their balance sheets. In this deal, bonds are collateralized by a future lease payment stream rather than the physical data center, resembling a collateralized debt obligation \(CDO\). Investors demand higher yields for such structures due to increased complexity and uncertainty about the underlying asset&\#x27;s value before completion.
