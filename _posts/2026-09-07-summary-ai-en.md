## [Tencent WeChat Vision Open-Sources Multimodal Embedding Model WeMM-Embedding](https://www.aibase.com/news/30866) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent&\#x27;s WeChat Vision Team has open-sourced WeMM-Embedding, a family of multimodal embedding models with 2B, 4B, and 9B variants supporting text and image inputs. The release claims production usage with a daily call volume exceeding ten billion. Open-sourcing a production-grade multimodal embedding model gives developers access to a proven, large-scale solution for multimodal retrieval, search, and RAG applications. The reported massive daily call volume also signals that such models can reliably operate under real-world demand. The 2B variant is built on Qwen3.5 and outputs 2,048-dimensional L2-normalized embeddings, accepting text, images, videos, visual documents, and interleaved multimodal inputs, though audio is not supported. The family shares a unified architecture and training strategy across all sizes.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 7, 12:01

**Background**: Multimodal embedding models map different data types, such as text and images, into a shared vector space so that semantically similar items are located close to each other regardless of their original modality. This shared representation enables tasks like image-text retrieval, semantic search, and multimodal question answering. WeMM-Embedding extends this concept beyond traditional text-image pairs to videos and visual documents, making it a general-purpose multimodal encoder.
