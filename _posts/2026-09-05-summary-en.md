## [Testing Shows DLSS 5 Raises RTX 5090 Power Draw by 34% at 4K](https://www.ithome.com/0/998/778.htm) ⭐️ 6.0/10

ComputerBase&\#x27;s testing in NBA 2K27 found that enabling DLSS 5 raises GPU power consumption, with more powerful cards seeing larger jumps. The RTX 5090 at 4K climbed from 417W to 561W—a 144W, or 34%, increase—while the RTX 5080 rose by 24%. This matters because DLSS has traditionally been framed as a performance booster that saves power, but DLSS 5&\#x27;s neural rendering instead significantly increases power draw. The findings could affect gamers&\#x27; cooling and PSU requirements, and shift how RTX 50-series cards are evaluated for real-world efficiency. The largest jump was on the RTX 5090, which went from 417W to 561W at 4K, a 34% increase; the RTX 5080 rose by 24%. These numbers come from a single game test, NBA 2K27, so results may vary across different titles and DLSS 5 modes.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 5, 10:49

**Background**: DLSS \(Deep Learning Super Sampling\) is a suite of real-time AI image enhancement and upscaling technologies developed by Nvidia for RTX GPUs. Older DLSS versions rendered at a lower resolution and used AI to upscale, improving frame rates with minimal quality loss. DLSS 5 goes further by introducing generative neural rendering, infusing pixels with photorealistic lighting and materials, which relies heavily on Tensor Cores and therefore increases GPU power consumption.
