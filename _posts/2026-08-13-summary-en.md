## [DeepMind&\#x27;s SL2T brings sign-language-to-text AI to Pixel 11](https://deepmind.google/blog/putting-sign-language-ai-into-users-hands/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Google DeepMind announced SL2T, a large-scale multilingual sign-language-to-text model, on August 12, 2026. The model debuts in consumer products with American Sign Language \(ASL\)-to-English translation on Pixel 11&\#x27;s Gboard and Live Transcribe, rolling out from August 20, 2026. This is a major accessibility milestone: sign language AI is now in the hands of Deaf and hard of hearing users on everyday devices. It signals a shift toward more inclusive AI, and strong zero-shot results suggest the approach can scale to more sign languages and devices. SL2T was trained on over 100,000 hours of data covering more than 50 sign languages, and achieves a zero-shot score of 70 BLEURT on the FLEURS-ASL benchmark. For privacy, it processes only hand and body pose keypoints rather than raw video; initial support is ASL-to-English, with more languages and devices planned.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 13, 08:55

**Background**: Sign language translation has historically been peripheral to mainstream machine translation research, partly because sign languages are visual rather than spoken. FLEURS-ASL, introduced in 2024, extended the multilingual FLEURS benchmark to include American Sign Language, enabling standardized evaluation. BLEURT is a learned evaluation metric that assesses translation quality based on fluency and meaning, and 70 BLEURT on FLEURS-ASL indicates near-human-quality output. SL2T&\#x27;s use of pose keypoints also reflects a privacy-conscious design choice for on-device accessibility AI.
