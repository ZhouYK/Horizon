## [World&\#x27;s largest single AI computing facility goes online in Inner Mongolia](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367666.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 6, Envision Group announced that the &\#x27;Vision Ulanqab Galaxy Base&\#x27; officially began operation. The facility is the world&\#x27;s largest single AI computing facility, with 120,000 square meters of building area, support for million-GPU parallel computing, and a planned total capacity of 2GW, with green electricity accounting for over 80%. This milestone shows how large-scale AI compute can be deployed, directly addressing the growing demand for AI training and inference. It also strengthens the &\#x27;East-Data-West-Computing&\#x27; strategy by leveraging Inner Mongolia&\#x27;s energy and cost advantages to support national AI development. Located about 240 kilometers from Beijing, Ulanqab offers a data transmission latency of only 4.2 milliseconds and electricity prices roughly 50% lower than in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region. The base is the first flagship project of Envision&\#x27;s &\#x27;Gobi Mission&\#x27; plan, aimed at providing a replicable solution for domestic computing clusters.

## [Musk Unveils SpaceX Lunar Factory Plan for AI Satellites](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/pure-insanity-elon-musk-details-173635969.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

In a first public earnings call, Elon Musk detailed a SpaceX plan to build a robotic factory on the Moon. The factory would use Starship to deliver equipment, extract metals like aluminum and titanium from lunar soil, and produce AI computing satellites launched into orbit via an electromagnetic mass driver. If realized, this plan would shift heavy manufacturing off Earth and drastically reduce the cost of deploying large AI satellite constellations. It also marks a major step toward establishing a permanent lunar economy, but the timeline is widely seen as highly speculative. During the earnings call, SpaceX reported quarterly revenue of $7.8 billion, while its space division lost $205 million due to Starship investment. Former SpaceX vice president Jim Cantrell called the idea &quot;pure insanity&quot; but said Musk could pull it off; the Moon&\#x27;s abrasive dust and 14-day light-dark cycles make the project extremely challenging.

**Background**: In situ resource utilization \(ISRU\) is the practice of using materials found on the Moon, Mars, or asteroids instead of bringing everything from Earth, a key concept for sustainable deep-space exploration. A mass driver is an electromagnetic launcher concept that could hurl payloads from a lunar surface into orbit without burning propellant, potentially acting as a low-cost &quot;catapult&quot; for space logistics. These technologies are considered essential for building infrastructure beyond Earth.

## [MiniMax H3 AMA: Open-Sourcing 2K Regeneration Model and Sparse Attention](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/s/fjM3d7AEV8) ⭐️ 7.0/10

In an AMA on r/StableDiffusion, the MiniMax H3 team announced plans to open-source H3-Regenerate-2K, a latent-space DiT regeneration model for high-resolution video generation, and to release a sparse attention reference implementation with no perceptible quality loss. They also confirmed improvements to address community-reported issues like Ref2VA quality degradation and blurred texture details. This signals a major open-source push for high-resolution video generation, potentially making state-of-the-art omni-modal generation more accessible to developers and researchers. Sparse attention could significantly cut inference costs for DiT-based video models, benefiting the broader generative AI ecosystem. H3-Regenerate-2K is specifically a dedicated latent-space DiT regeneration model, not an ordinary super-resolution upscaler; no concrete release date was given. The team is also exploring 4/8-step low-step versions and a standalone image generation model derived from the H3 model family.

**Background**: MiniMax H3 is a general-purpose, omni-modal generative system that can understand text, images, video, and audio, and generates video with native stereo audio at up to 2K resolution and 15 seconds. DiT \(Diffusion Transformer\) models replace the U-Net backbone with a transformer operating on latent space, and sparse attention methods aim to speed up the slow 3D Full Attention mechanism in video diffusion models.
