On November 14, 2026, China&\#x27;s 2026 edition of the national basic medical insurance and commercial health insurance innovative drug catalog was released, and WuXi JW Therapeutics&\#x27; \(02126.HK\) relma-cel injection \(brand name BENDA\) was listed at ¥288,600 per treatment — the first and so far only CAR-T product covered by basic medical insurance, a cut of more than 77% from its original ¥1.29 million price. The ¥288,600 price is reportedly the lowest for any CAR-T product globally, and relma-cel is a CD19-targeted autologous CAR-T therapy approved in China in 2021 for relapsed or refractory large B-cell lymphoma. The critical variable is the company&\#x27;s guidance that full-scope production cost will drop more than 65% by 2027 compared with 2025; some observers have questioned whether the company is effectively &quot;losing money to make noise&quot;, a charge management explicitly rejected.

**Background**: CAR-T \(chimeric antigen receptor T-cell\) therapy works by collecting a patient&\#x27;s own T cells, genetically engineering them to express a chimeric antigen receptor that recognizes a specific tumor antigen such as CD19, expanding them outside the body, and infusing them back so the modified cells kill cancer cells. rss · FT Markets · Nov 17, 17:30

**Background**: A convertible bond is debt that the issuer can settle either in cash or by handing over shares, usually at a preset conversion price; if the stock trades above that price the bond is &quot;in the money&quot; and holders prefer shares, which increases the share count and dilutes existing owners. Several companies have recently repositioned themselves as &quot;bitcoin treasury&quot; vehicles, issuing cheap convertible debt to buy bitcoin and betting that the stock rises faster than the debt cost. That model works well in bull markets but turns fragile when the share price falls, since the notes then revert to being ordinary debt that must be repaid.

## [Trump administration proposes easing ERISA pension exemptions for firms with misconduct ties](https://www.ft.com/content/fe6861a1-cc15-4fce-8a8d-7c3fdce2b022?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 6.0/10

The Trump administration is proposing to streamline the process by which investment firms with links to past misconduct obtain exemptions from ERISA&\#x27;s prohibited-transaction rules, while also granting those firms a 10-year reprieve from scrutiny over that wrongdoing. A senior Labor Department official told the FT that the change would apply retroactively to nearly all of the roughly 30 institutions that have already sought and received individual exemptions. The proposal would loosen restrictions that were tightened during the Biden administration in 2024, when the Labor Department broadened the circumstances under which pension managers could be barred from overseeing retirement savings because of misconduct elsewhere in their corporate group. It would materially affect asset managers seeking access to US retirement plan money, and could reduce the compliance burden and reputational consequences of corporate misconduct for large financial groups. Under current ERISA rules, any pension manager with a connection to a company that committed misconduct must request a special individual exemption from the Labor Department; the new proposal would simplify that process and create a 10-year moratorium from scrutiny for managers granted an exemption. The measure is still a proposal rather than a final rule, and ERISA&\#x27;s definition of misconduct remains broadly written, so the practical scope of the relief will depend on how the final text defines disqualifying conduct.

rss · FT Markets · Nov 17, 11:00

**Background**: ERISA, the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, governs most US private-sector workplace retirement and health plans and bars certain dealings between plans and &quot;parties in interest&quot; such as service providers and fiduciaries. Because some otherwise necessary transactions would be caught by these prohibitions, the law allows the Department of Labor to grant prohibited transaction exemptions \(PTEs\), either individual or class-wide, after an application and review process. Historically, the department has used this exemption power as a lever over firms whose corporate affiliates have run afoul of the law, requiring disclosures and conditions before granting relief.

Japan has proposed creating an Asian counterpart to the International Energy Agency to jointly manage strategic oil reserves and shield import-dependent Asia from energy shocks caused by war or climate change. The plan, floated by Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi at a meeting of the Asia Zero Emissions Community, would have Japan help lead an Asian oil security task force alongside the Indonesia-based Economic Research Institute for ASEAN and East Asia \(ERIA\) to draft rules for emergency releases and sharing of oil from national stockpiles. Asia is the world&\#x27;s largest oil-importing region but lacks the kind of coordinated emergency stockpile mechanism that IEA members use to stabilize markets during supply disruptions. If advanced, the proposal could change how Asian countries respond to Middle East supply shocks and reduce their vulnerability to price spikes, though it would sit outside the existing IEA framework. rss · FT Markets · Nov 17, 07:01

**Background**: The International Energy Agency, founded in 1974 after the first oil crisis, requires its members to hold emergency stockpiles equivalent to at least 90 days of net oil imports and coordinates collective releases during supply disruptions. Its membership is dominated by advanced economies in Europe, North America and Japan, with major Asian importers such as China and India outside the club. Strategic petroleum reserves are government-controlled crude stockpiles, like the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve — the world&\#x27;s largest publicly known emergency supply, with capacity of about 714 million barrels.

## [Bridgewater, Soros Cut Nvidia Stakes in Q3 as Wall Street Splits on AI](https://www.caixin.com/2026-11-17/102505969.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

SEC 13F filings released on November 16 show that Bridgewater Associates cut its Nvidia position by about 30.5% in the third quarter, reducing its holding from 3.8662 million shares at the end of June to roughly 2.6882 million shares as of September 30. Soros Fund Management also trimmed its Nvidia stake and Renaissance Technologies nearly exited the stock, while some other hedge funds added to their positions over the same period. The split in positioning shows Nvidia is no longer a default consensus holding for large institutions, a notable sentiment signal as the stock declines and the company&\#x27;s next earnings report approaches. Divergent 13F flows from marquee names like Bridgewater and Soros can influence how retail and smaller institutional investors read the durability of the AI trade. Even after the cut, Bridgewater&\#x27;s roughly 2.69 million Nvidia shares were worth about $614 million at quarter-end, equal to around 2.7% of its disclosed U.S. equity portfolio and still its third-largest holding. The filings also show some capital rotating toward the upstream and midstream of the AI supply chain, and Chinese-linked managers making differing choices.

rss · 财新网 · Nov 17, 15:23

**Background**: Form 13F is a quarterly disclosure required by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission from institutional managers overseeing at least $100 million in qualifying securities, listing their long equity positions. Because filings are due 45 days after each quarter ends, the data reflects positioning as of September 30 and can be well over three months stale by the time investors read it, so it is a sentiment and flow signal rather than a real-time trading cue. Note also that Bridgewater is a hedge fund while Soros Fund Management is structured as a family office, two different vehicles that both fall under the same disclosure rule. Nvidia has been the bellwether of the AI infrastructure boom, making institutional shifts in its shareholder base closely watched.
