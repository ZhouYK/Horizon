## [Vara Receives World&\#x27;s First Autonomous Breast Cancer Screening AI Certification](https://www.aibase.com/news/30840) ⭐️ 9.0/10

German company Vara has received EU MDR Class IIb CE certification for its breast cancer screening AI, making it the world&\#x27;s first certified autonomous triage AI for screening. The AI can rule out clearly normal mammograms without radiologist review, while other cases are still verified by doctors. This is the first regulatory certification for autonomous AI triage in breast cancer screening, marking a significant milestone for AI in medical diagnostics. It could ease radiologist workloads and influence how screening programs in Europe and beyond adopt AI. Under the EU MDR, Class IIb is a higher-risk device category that requires notified-body involvement and a rigorous conformity assessment. The certification is limited to organized population screening and does not apply to patients with symptoms or to diagnostic examinations.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 4, 15:01

**Background**: CE marking under the EU Medical Device Regulation \(MDR 2017/745\) confirms that a product meets EU safety and performance requirements. Devices are classified by risk, with Class IIb covering moderate-to-high-risk products that require independent assessment. In breast cancer screening, AI triage tools aim to reduce radiologists&\#x27; workload by identifying low-risk mammograms; recent paired noninferiority trials suggest such workflows can maintain safety while skipping radiologist reading for low-risk cases.

## [Google DeepMind releases WeatherNext 3 for hourly 5-km global AI forecasts](https://www.aibase.com/news/30844) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google DeepMind and Google Research have released WeatherNext 3, an AI weather model that ingests live global geostationary satellite mosaics and station observations to produce hourly-updated global forecasts at 0.05° \(~5 km\) resolution. It tackles two key weaknesses of existing AI and numerical weather models: coarse resolution that poorly represents local terrain and a roughly six-hour data initialization lag. If verified, faster, higher-resolution forecasts could improve severe-weather warnings, renewable-energy planning, and downstream products across Google. WeatherNext 3 updates every hour instead of every six hours, uses the functional generative network \(FGN\) approach introduced with WeatherNext 2, and trains directly on raw station observations rather than NWP model output. The developers say this direct ingestion reduces biases for fast-changing variables such as rain and surface temperature.

aibase · AIbase · Sep 4, 16:01

**Background**: Most AI weather models, including DeepMind&\#x27;s earlier WeatherNext 2, are trained on outputs of numerical weather prediction \(NWP\) models—supercomputer physics simulations that are updated roughly every six hours, which can make them slow and biased for rapidly changing weather. Geostationary weather satellites continuously observe the Earth, and when multiple satellites&\#x27; observations are stitched into a global mosaic they provide a current, detailed view of the atmosphere. WeatherNext 3 feeds this real-time satellite mosaic and in-situ station data directly into a generative model, a class of AI that learns to produce realistic global weather fields, allowing frequent re-initializations.
