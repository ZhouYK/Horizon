This warning from a top Huawei scientist strikes at the heart of the current AI hardware boom, since Nvidia&\#x27;s dominance rests on continuously scaling chips and memory. **Background**: The background here is Moore&\#x27;s Law, the long-observed trend that transistors on a chip double roughly every two years, enabling ever-cheaper and more powerful processors. ## [Google builds $200B Wall Street financing machine for Anthropic AI chips](https://www.ft.com/content/549f2e23-5aa2-49c7-9ea6-a9784ab7087c) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google has quietly assembled a roughly $200 billion financing architecture to deliver more than $150 billion in AI chips to Anthropic, with the first deals completed in June through a special purpose vehicle called Compute SPV. The initial transactions purchased about $35 billion in hardware, equivalent to 1 gigawatt of compute and 1 million TPUs. This is one of the largest infrastructure financing structures ever created, showing how tech giants are shifting massive AI capital expenditure off their balance sheets and onto Wall Street. It underscores the extraordinary scale of the AI arms race and could set a template for funding future AI compute projects. Participants include Broadcom, Apollo, Blackstone, Morgan Stanley, and several crypto-mining companies, according to the Financial Times. Because Anthropic has no credit rating, the risk is shared: Google guarantees the data centers, Broadcom buys and helps finance chips, while Apollo and Blackstone purchase hardware and lease it back to Anthropic.

**Background**: A special purpose vehicle \(SPV\) is a legal entity created for a specific financial or operational purpose, commonly used in project finance to isolate risk. Vendor financing is an arrangement in which a supplier provides financing to a buyer, a model popularized by Boeing and GE for selling aircraft and engines. TPUs \(Tensor Processing Units\) are Google&\#x27;s custom application-specific integrated circuits \(ASICs\) designed for neural network and machine learning workloads. This financing structure lets the involved parties avoid keeping hundreds of billions of dollars in AI hardware on their own balance sheets.

## [China Issues First Mandatory Safety Standard for L3/L4 Self-Driving Cars](https://wap.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/zbys/qcgy/art/2026/art_a1d2072374884287b67048a77560014e.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 30, 2026, China&\#x27;s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology \(MIIT\) released the mandatory national standard GB 44721—2026, titled &quot;Intelligent Connected Vehicles—Safety Requirements for Automated Driving Systems.&quot; The standard takes effect on July 1, 2027, and is China&\#x27;s first mandatory safety standard for L3 \(conditionally automated\) and L4 \(highly automated\) driving systems. This marks a major regulatory shift from recommended to mandatory safety requirements for L3/L4 autonomous vehicles in China. It establishes a legal safety baseline that automakers and technology developers must meet, significantly influencing the commercialization and deployment of autonomous driving in the world&\#x27;s largest auto market. The standard applies to M-class \(passenger\) and N-class \(cargo\) vehicles equipped with L3 or L4 automated driving systems, but it does not cover automated parking systems. It builds a safety framework around four dimensions: enterprise full-life-cycle safety assurance, dynamic driving capabilities, human-machine interaction and user notification, and multi-dimensional inspection and testing, requiring automated driving systems to be at least as safe as a competent, attentive human driver.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 4, 13:06

**Background**: Autonomous driving is categorized into levels from L0 to L5; L3 allows the system to drive under certain conditions while the driver must remain ready to take over, and L4 allows the system to drive without driver intervention within defined operational design domains. In vehicle classification, M-class vehicles are passenger vehicles and N-class are cargo vehicles. The new GB 44721—2026 replaces the earlier recommended national standard issued in 2024, representing a systematic upgrade from voluntary to mandatory compliance in China&\#x27;s autonomous driving regulation.

Apple has approved Microsoft&\#x27;s Digital Markets Act \(DMA\) interoperability request to bring cross-device clipboard sharing between iPhone and Windows PCs to EU users with iOS 28, expected in fall 2027. Microsoft submitted the request on March 25, 2026, and it was approved on June 26, 2026. Apple says the implementation resembles the accessory notification framework introduced in iOS 26.5, with developers using AccessorySetupKit to handle a one-time pairing authorization. The feature is currently planned only for EU users, and Apple has not confirmed it will make the first official iOS 28 release.
