## [Mistral Launches Agentic Search with Multi-Step Retrieval, Boosting Complex Document Accuracy to 86%](https://www.aibase.com/news/30529) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Mistral AI has officially launched Agentic Search, an upgraded solution featuring a multi-step retrieval loop that iteratively finds, inspects, and verifies information across data sources. According to the announcement, it improves accuracy on complex documents to 86%. Traditional single-pass RAG systems struggle with complex long documents and cross-source comparisons because they lack iterative reasoning. This launch directly addresses a key limitation in enterprise AI, making it easier for companies to extract insights from confidential financial reports, legal contracts, and other long-form documents. Agentic Search introduces a multi-step retrieval loop that breaks complex queries into subqueries and refines context based on intermediate results. The reported 86% accuracy improvement targets structured documents and cross-source comparison tasks, which are particularly challenging for traditional retrieval-augmented generation.

aibase · AIbase · Aug 21, 15:29

**Background**: Retrieval-Augmented Generation \(RAG\) combines a language model with an external knowledge source, retrieving relevant text chunks to ground generated answers. Traditional RAG performs a single retrieval pass, which limits its ability to handle tasks that require reasoning across multiple sections or documents. Agentic search, also known as agentic retrieval, uses LLMs to decompose complex queries into subqueries and iterate until the information is found and verified. This approach is part of a broader trend toward AI agents that can plan, act, and refine their research process.

## [LFM2.5 DSpark Draft Models Speed Up Inference 3.18x](https://www.aibase.com/news/30528) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Liquid AI and Hugging Face released three LFM2.5 DSpark draft checkpoints \(1.2B, 2.6B, 8B-A1B\) that add a speculative decoding path. The new draft models boost inference throughput up to 3.18x on GPUs and edge devices without changing output quality. Speculative decoding is a practical way to cut LLM inference latency, and these official draft checkpoints make it easy for developers to adopt. This release benefits the LLM inference community by improving throughput on both high-end GPUs like H100 and on-device models, without sacrificing output quality. The 2.6B DSpark model is designed as an on-device agentic model, and benchmarks show acceptance rates and speedups measured with SGLang on 1xH100 and llama.cpp with Metal on M4 Max. The technique uses a small draft model to propose candidate tokens that a larger target model verifies in a single forward pass, preserving the original output distribution.

aibase · AIbase · Aug 21, 15:29

**Background**: Speculative decoding is an inference-time optimization for autoregressive LLMs that generates multiple tokens per step instead of one. It works by having a smaller draft model propose a sequence of candidate tokens, which the larger target model then verifies in a single forward pass using modified rejection sampling. Because verification preserves the target model&\#x27;s original output distribution, the technique produces the same results as standard decoding while cutting latency by roughly two to three times, similar to speculative execution in CPUs.

## [Pew Research: Over 30% of Post-ChatGPT Webpages Contain AI-Generated Content](https://www.aibase.com/news/30521) ⭐️ 7.5/10

A Pew Research Center study released August 20 found that more than one-third of English-language webpages published after ChatGPT&\#x27;s launch show signs of AI-generated or AI-edited text. The analysis leveraged the Open Pangram detector on about 500,000 Common Crawl pages, with a random sample of 10,000 pages showing around 10% with clear AI traces. This is one of the first large-scale empirical measurements of how quickly generative AI has permeated the online information ecosystem. It has significant implications for search quality, content authenticity, and the trustworthiness of web information. The phrase &\#x27;after ChatGPT&\#x27;s launch&\#x27; refers to content published after November 30, 2022, when ChatGPT debuted. The 10% AI-trace figure for the random sample reflects that many sampled pages predate ChatGPT, so the actual proportion among post-launch pages is over 30%.

aibase · AIbase · Aug 21, 11:29

**Background**: ChatGPT, released by OpenAI in late 2022, made generative AI widely accessible and quickly became a tool for writing and editing web content. Common Crawl is a free, open repository of web crawl data that researchers use for large-scale web analysis. Open Pangram is an AI detector that can identify text generated by major language models such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
