## [FAA Halts All U.S. Departing Flights After Communications Outage](https://international.caixin.com/2026-11-14/102504717.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Starting around 4 p.m. Eastern Time on November 13, 2026, the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration issued a nationwide ground stop barring all departing flights after a communications failure affected every air route traffic control center in the country; flights already airborne were allowed to continue, and inbound international flights were handled case by case. The ground stop was later lifted the same evening, with Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy describing intermittent radar and communications outages and blaming aging equipment, while DHS Secretary Mullin stressed at a White House briefing that it was a &quot;telecom interruption&quot; and not &quot;another 9/11.&quot; This is only the second time in U.S. history that all departing flights have been grounded nationwide, the first being the September 11, 2001 attacks, so the order rippled instantly through airlines, airports, connecting itineraries and same-day business travel across the country. It also raises pointed questions about the resilience and age of the air traffic control infrastructure that the entire U.S. aviation system depends on. A ground stop requires aircraft to stay on the ground rather than diverting or slowing traffic in the air, which is why flights already en route were unaffected while departures were frozen. Officials said the failures were intermittent rather than a single clean break, the root cause is still under investigation, and no evidence of malicious activity has been reported.

rss · 财新网 · Nov 14, 15:06

**Background**: In the U.S., high-altitude traffic between airport approaches and departures is handled by air route traffic control centers \(ARTCCs\), often called &quot;centers,&quot; each covering a large block of airspace; the FAA runs roughly two dozen of them, and they hand traffic off to one another and to terminal radar facilities. Because these centers coordinate seamlessly across the country, a communications problem affecting all of them at once can halt departures nationwide even if individual airports are functioning normally. A ground stop is a distinct tool from a ground delay program: it keeps aircraft on the ground for a specific destination or region until controllers can safely accept them.

## [CSRC&\#x27;s Wu Qing Flags Research on IPO Rules for Large, New-Tech Firms](https://finance.caixin.com/2026-11-14/102504948.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Speaking at the inaugural China Capital Market Academic Conference on November 14, 2026, CSRC Chairman Wu Qing said the regulator will strengthen research on how listing rules can better fit large and new-type technology companies, including risk-response mechanisms, and will study optimizing quantitative trading and share-reduction rules to curb &quot;herd effects&quot; and short-term trading volatility. He also pledged deeper study of support for listings by quality firms in new consumption and traditional industry upgrading to improve the listed-company structure. The remarks signal possible shifts in both A-share IPO supply and the regulatory treatment of quantitative strategies and shareholder selling, which could affect tech issuers seeking listings, quant funds, and controlling shareholders planning reductions. Because these are research directions from the top securities regulator, they carry weight as an early indicator of future rule-making even before any draft measures appear. The speech outlined research directions rather than concrete measures, with no specific figures, timelines or finalized rules disclosed. The IPO discussion is framed around &quot;scientific grasp of pace,&quot; suggesting any accommodation for large tech issuers would be calibrated against market conditions rather than implemented as a blanket liberalization.

rss · 财新网 · Nov 14, 11:49

**Background**: A-shares are RMB-denominated shares of mainland Chinese companies traded on the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges, and the market is heavily influenced by domestic retail investors. China already requires all investors, including quant traders, to trade on a T+1 basis and has restricted securities lending used for disguised T+0 since 2024, while the CSRC issued interim rules in May 2024 governing share reductions by listed-company shareholders. Wu&\#x27;s remarks extend these existing regulatory threads toward large technology listings and volatility control.
