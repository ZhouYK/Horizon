If completed, the deal would make Physical Intelligence one of the most valuable AI robotics companies in the world and signal that investors see general-purpose robot foundation models as the next major frontier after language and image models, setting a benchmark that will influence valuations across the embodied-AI sector. The talks are described as early-stage, so terms, the final amount raised and the valuation could still change or the round may not close; the company&\#x27;s core technology is a family of Vision-Language-Action \(VLA\) foundation models, including π₀, π₀.5 and π₀.7, trained on data from many different robots as well as human demonstrations.

google\_news · Bloomberg.com · Oct 27, 19:43

**Background**: Physical Intelligence \(often abbreviated π\) was founded in 2024 by veterans of Google DeepMind, OpenAI and UC Berkeley, and is backed by investors including OpenAI, Jeff Bezos and Thrive Capital. Vision-Language-Action models are the robotics analogue of large language models — they take camera images and text instructions as input and output motor commands — and the field&\#x27;s central bet is that data scale, not hand-written control code, will deliver general-purpose physical intelligence.
