## [US Rolls Out Section 301 &\#x27;Overcapacity&\#x27; Tariffs on 14 Trade Partners](https://international.caixin.com/2026-11-26/102508942.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The U.S. Trade Representative announced on November 25, 2026 the results of its second Section 301 investigation, this one into industrial overcapacity, imposing new tariffs on 14 trading partners including China and the EU. The tariffs affect a wide swath of U.S. imports and major economies, reshaping the post-IEEPA tariff landscape and keeping upward pressure on U.S. effective tariff rates. rss · 财新网 · Nov 26, 22:56

**Background**: Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 lets the U.S. president, through the USTR, investigate and retaliate against foreign trade practices deemed unfair or discriminatory, including through tariffs. Section 122 of the same 1974 law permits a temporary balance-of-payments surcharge of up to 15% for 150 days, which is why the administration has rotated through different legal authorities. The &\#x27;effective tariff rate&\#x27; is the average tariff actually paid on imports, weighted by trade volumes.
