Indonesia&\#x27;s mining sector is largely governed by long-term contracts between the state and investors, which set out ownership, tax and revenue-sharing terms; changing those terms for new projects is a way for Jakarta to raise revenue without breaching existing agreements. The 70% target is unusually high by international standards, which is why the choice between an equity stake and a revenue share matters so much for investors.
