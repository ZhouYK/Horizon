The update is significant because DRG/DIP now covers hospitals nationwide and directly determines how inpatient care is reimbursed, influencing treatment choices, drug pricing, and hospital finances. Expanding room for new drugs is especially relevant to pharmaceutical innovation and market access in China. There are two coexisting mechanisms: DRG classifies patients into a relatively small number of resource-similar groups and imposes stronger incentives for standardized treatment and cost control, while DIP uses big data to cluster cases by &quot;primary diagnosis plus primary operation&quot; and settles according to disease scores. Version 3.0 also reportedly adds nationally recommended primary-care \(&quot;grassroots&quot;\) disease groups, an institutional arrangement first introduced at the national level.

rss · 财新网 · Sep 3, 11:54

**Background**: China&\#x27;s medical insurance historically reimbursed hospitals item by item \(for example, per CT scan or ultrasound\), which can encourage excessive testing and treatment. Under DRG/DIP &quot;packaged payment,&quot; the insurer pays a fixed amount per disease group or disease score; hospitals keep savings and absorb overspending. The reform has been under way for about four years and now covers all regions and qualifying hospitals; a version 2.0 scheme was issued in July 2024 before this 3.0 update. Keeping the groupings close to real-world clinical needs remains a continuing challenge.
