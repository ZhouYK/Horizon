## [South Korea Picks Three Consortiums for Free National AI Service](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260828/skt-kt-kakao-consortiums-selected-for-free-ai-service-for-public) ⭐️ 7.0/10

South Korea&\#x27;s Ministry of Science and ICT selected three consortiums led by SK Telecom, KT, and Kakao to operate the &\#x27;AI for All&\#x27; project, offering token-unlimited free AI service to all citizens. Pilot testing starts in September, with a full launch before the end of the year. This marks a major policy push to make domestic large language models a public utility, giving every citizen free access to AI tools for everyday tasks. It also strengthens South Korea&\#x27;s AI ecosystem against global rivals, with the government subsidizing infrastructure and operations. The government will provide 512 NVIDIA B200 GPUs to the three consortiums and subsidize nationwide operating costs from 2027. The service will connect to government systems for hospital appointments, housing searches, and tax consultations; Naver is not participating.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 29, 15:31

**Background**: Large language models \(LLMs\) process text using tokens, which are small units of data split from larger chunks of information; many paid AI services charge by token usage. &\#x27;AI for All&\#x27; removes this barrier by offering token-unlimited access. The NVIDIA B200, based on the Blackwell architecture, features 192GB HBM3e memory and is designed for training and inference of such models.

## [Geekerwan: Tensor G6 on TSMC 3nm Only Matches Older Snapdragon Levels](https://www.bilibili.com/opus/1241599904882622480) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Geekerwan published an early benchmark test of Google&\#x27;s upcoming Tensor G6 chip, reporting that its CPU performance matches the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and its GPU matches the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, despite being built on TSMC&\#x27;s 3nm process and slated for 2026. This matters because Google&\#x27;s Tensor chips are central to Pixel phones; if a 2026 chip made on TSMC&\#x27;s advanced 3nm node only matches Qualcomm&\#x27;s earlier flagship tiers, Pixel devices may continue to trail in raw performance and efficiency. It also raises questions about Google&\#x27;s chip design strategy. The test specifically targets power efficiency and compares against Qualcomm&\#x27;s Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 \(CPU\) and 8 Gen 2 \(GPU\). According to Geekerwan, the Tensor G6 is a 2026 chip, meaning it lags roughly two generations behind Qualcomm&\#x27;s current flagship SoCs in these preliminary benchmarks.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 29, 10:30

**Background**: Google&\#x27;s Tensor series is a custom ARM-based SoC used in Pixel smartphones, designed to focus on AI and machine-learning tasks rather than raw performance. Earlier Tensor generations used Samsung&\#x27;s foundry process and often drew criticism for heat and battery life. TSMC&\#x27;s 3nm \(N3\) node is a leading-edge FinFET process that offers better performance, power, and density, making the choice significant for efficiency.
