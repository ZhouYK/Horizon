## [BESIII Collaboration Confirms Glueball Existence After 15 Years](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Chinese-led BESIII collaboration has confirmed the existence of glueballs, a new form of matter, for the first time. After 15 years of research, they identified the particle X\(2370\) as being glueball-dominated, based on 2024 measurements of its quantum numbers and flavor-singlet properties. This is a major experimental validation of the Standard Model and quantum chromodynamics, confirming a particle composed entirely of force carriers \(gluons\) for the first time. It fills a long-standing gap in particle physics and strengthens our understanding of the strong interaction. X\(2370\) was first discovered in J/ψ decays at BEPCII in 2011, and in 2024 the collaboration used a sample of 10 billion J/ψ particles to determine its spin-parity quantum numbers as 0⁻⁺. The new study found multiple new decay modes and confirmed its flavor-singlet nature, but the result indicates X\(2370\) is &\#x27;glueball-dominated&\#x27; rather than a pure glueball.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 07:31

**Background**: In particle physics, a glueball is a hypothetical composite particle consisting solely of gluons, with no valence quarks. Gluons carry color charge and interact strongly with each other, so they can bind together. BESIII is a general-purpose detector at the Beijing Electron Positron Collider \(BEPCII\), and its huge samples of J/ψ decays, which are gluon-rich, make it an ideal place to search for glueballs.
