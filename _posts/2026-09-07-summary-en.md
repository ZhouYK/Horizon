## [Huawei Unveils Kirin 9050 Pro With Logic Folding Technology](https://www.news.cn/20260907/adf46c5c003240d28cc3cf6de54f9b5f/c.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Huawei announced the Kirin 9050 Pro chip at the Mate XT 2 launch event in Guangzhou, marking its return to flagship-grade processors after a six-year gap. The chip is described as the first high-performance processor to use logic folding technology. If logic folding delivers real performance gains through 3D stacking rather than transistor shrinking, it could provide an alternative path to Moore&\#x27;s law scaling. That would have broad implications for the semiconductor industry, with some commentators suggesting it could challenge incumbents like ASML. The chip stacks logic units inside a single package with vertical interconnect channels to shorten signal paths, reportedly cutting typical core connection lengths by about 20% and critical paths by up to 70%. He Tingbo, president of Huawei&\#x27;s semiconductor business, also published a paper on ChinaXiv that used mass-production chip measurement data to rebut doubts about heat in 3D-stacked chips.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 7, 08:20

**Background**: Logic folding is a chip design approach tied to Huawei&\#x27;s stated &\#x27;τ \(Tau\) law&\#x27;: it re-plans the circuit layout at the design stage and folds two-dimensional logic paths into a three-dimensional stacking architecture. This differs from chiplet packaging or conventional 3D stacking, which typically assemble existing dies, and from traditional scaling that relies on shrinking transistor dimensions.
