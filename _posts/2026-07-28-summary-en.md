## [Exchanges Mandate WAN Market Data Lines with 2ms Latency Floor](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/ba7Rx5VCnYnzJzWMHyLoaQ) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Chinese stock exchanges have issued a technical requirement mandating all brokers to switch from LAN-based market data lines to WAN-based lines, with a minimum round-trip latency of 2ms. Existing LAN lines will be closed by the end of this month. This regulatory change fundamentally alters the market data infrastructure for all Chinese brokers, directly impacting latency-sensitive trading strategies such as high-frequency trading. The mandatory latency floor may reduce the speed advantage of firms with proximity to exchange servers, potentially leveling the playing field but also increasing systemic latency. The new requirement applies to both existing and new WAN lines used for trading and market data services, mandating a minimum round-trip latency of 2ms. Brokers must comply by the end of July, when all LAN-based lines will be shut down.

**Background**: In financial trading, market data latency is critical for profitability, especially in high-frequency trading where even microsecond delays can affect execution. LAN connections offer lower latency and higher speed compared to WAN, as they cover shorter physical distances. Exchanges typically provide both LAN and WAN access, but LAN lines allow certain brokers to obtain data faster. The new mandate standardizes connectivity to WAN and imposes a minimum latency, likely aimed at reducing technological disparities among market participants.

## [Cloudflare Q2 2026 Internet Disruptions: Natural Disasters and Government Actions Lead](https://blog.cloudflare.com/q2-2026-internet-disruption-summary/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Cloudflare published its Q2 2026 Internet disruption summary, highlighting natural disasters and government actions as the primary causes of major outages worldwide. Notable events include Typhoon Sinlaku in Guam, Iran&\#x27;s 88-day internet shutdown, and a DNSSEC key error affecting .de domains. This quarterly report provides a global perspective on internet resilience and fragility, showing that both natural forces and deliberate government actions can severely disrupt connectivity. It underscores the need for robust infrastructure and careful management of critical systems like DNSSEC. During the outage in Guam, traffic dropped 80% below expected levels. Germany&\#x27;s .de DNSSEC key update introduced an incorrect signature, causing global validating resolvers to reject all .de domains temporarily. Iran&\#x27;s internet was restored after 88 days but only returned to pre-shutdown traffic levels.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 28, 15:21

**Background**: DNSSEC \(Domain Name System Security Extensions\) adds cryptographic signatures to DNS data to prevent spoofing and tampering. When a DNSSEC key is misconfigured or produces invalid signatures, validating resolvers will treat the affected domains as insecure and refuse to serve their records, causing widespread outages. The Domain Name System \(DNS\) is essential for translating human-readable domain names into IP addresses, so any disruption to DNS can make websites and services unreachable.
