## [Memory Chip Value Per Area Overtakes Leading-Edge Logic Chips](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/dram/dram-is-now-more-expensive-than-compute-chips-on-per-area-basis-ai-demand-drives-memory-die-value-past-leading-edge-silicon) ⭐️ 8.0/10

According to a Tom&\#x27;s Hardware report, AI-driven demand for high-bandwidth memory \(HBM\) has pushed the value of memory dies per unit of silicon area above that of leading-edge logic chips, a reversal of the traditional semiconductor hierarchy in which advanced-node processors were the most valuable product per area. This shift means memory makers are moving from commodity suppliers to critical, high-margin players in the AI chip supply chain, which could reshape pricing power, capacity allocation and strategic priorities across the semiconductor industry as AI infrastructure spending keeps expanding. HBM achieves its value density because it requires complex 3D die stacking, advanced packaging techniques such as 2.5D/3D integration and through-silicon vias, and much tighter yield control than conventional DRAM, all of which raise cost per unit area well above that of planar logic silicon.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 23, 11:39

**Background**: High Bandwidth Memory \(HBM\) is a memory interface built on 3D-stacked synchronous DRAM, originally developed by Samsung and AMD, that uses an exceptionally wide data path to deliver very high bandwidth while reducing the distance and energy needed to move data. It is paired with AI accelerators such as GPUs because large AI models are often limited by how fast data can be fed to the compute dies. Advanced packaging refers to techniques that combine multiple chips into one module, and yield refers to the share of manufactured dies that pass quality tests — both are crucial because stacking many DRAM layers multiplies the cost of any defect.

## [ShinyHunters Claims It Breached FBI, Stole Employee Data](https://www.404media.co/we-hacked-the-fbi-hackers-say-they-have-data-on-all-fbi-employees/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The extortion group ShinyHunters claims it breached multiple FBI-related services and exfiltrated personal data on all FBI employees and job applicants, providing reporters with a sample of roughly 5,000 alleged employee records that include names, home addresses, phone numbers, and details about family members such as spouses. The FBI has not confirmed the claim, and no independent verification of the full data set has been published. If the data is genuine, exposed home addresses and family details could be used to track, harass, or threaten FBI employees and their relatives, creating serious security and counterintelligence risks for US law enforcement and intelligence operations. The claim also underscores how recruitment portals and third-party services can become a soft entry point into otherwise well-defended government agencies. The publicly shown evidence is only a partial sample of about 5,000 records, so the claim to hold data on &quot;all&quot; FBI employees and applicants remains unproven. Reporting indicates the breach is linked to the bureau&\#x27;s recruitment site FBIjobs.gov rather than internal FBI systems, and ShinyHunters is known as a financially motivated extortion group rather than a state-sponsored actor.

**Background**: ShinyHunters is a black-hat criminal hacking and extortion group active since around 2019, linked to numerous large-scale data breaches and known for stealing data and then demanding payment to keep it private. The project has been explored for several months and has moved into prototyping, with support from executives including Tim Cook, though no launch decision has been made. Apple entering a screenless, band-only form factor would mark a break from the Apple Watch&\#x27;s display-centric approach and directly challenge Whoop&\#x27;s subscription model in the recovery and performance-tracking niche. If it ships, it could shift how mainstream wearables are designed and monetized, pressuring rivals in both hardware and subscription health services. The device is still in early technical research and no release has been confirmed; if Apple proceeds, the earliest possible launch is around 2028. The reported design uses a thin fabric band with integrated sensors rather than a display, which would mean health data is surfaced through a companion app instead of on-wrist.

## [Anonymous Model Space Bunny Alpha Launches Free on OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/stealth/space-bunny-alpha) ⭐️ 6.0/10

An anonymous third-party model named Space Bunny Alpha appeared on OpenRouter on September 23, 2026, offered for free while in preview. It advertises fast inference, strong coding ability, native multimodal input, and a 1M-token context window, but its developer is not disclosed. Stealth, unattributed models on OpenRouter are frequently a signal that a lab is A/B testing an unreleased frontier model in public, so free access lets developers probe new capabilities before any official launch. It also adds competitive pressure on pricing and context length across the hosted-model market, even though nothing here is verified by benchmarks. The 1M-token context window is in line with what frontier models such as Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 now offer generally, but a large advertised window does not guarantee usable long-context performance. Because the model is explicitly in preview with no vendor attribution, rate limits, availability and stability remain unclear and outputs may change without notice.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 23, 15:42

**Background**: OpenRouter is a routing service that exposes hundreds of LLMs behind a single API, adding features such as fallback behavior and cost governance, which makes it a common venue for quietly testing experimental models. A context window is the total amount of text — measured in tokens, roughly fragments of words — that a model can consider at once across input and output. &quot;Native multimodal&quot; means one unified architecture processes image, text \(and sometimes audio\) tokens end-to-end in a shared representation, rather than attaching separate adapters to a text-only model.
